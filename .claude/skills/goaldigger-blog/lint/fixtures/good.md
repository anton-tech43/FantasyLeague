# Leeds United for beginners

Leeds are fifth. He told me this twice before I'd put my bag down.

I asked what fifth meant, and he said it was good, but not as good as he'd like it to be, which I'm told is the permanent state of being a Leeds fan. Nine points from five games. Two wins, three draws, no losses (I checked, because he'd gone quiet on the last one).

Next up is Arsenal, and this is where his face does a thing.

Arsenal have won the last five times these two have met. The score across those games is 16 to 2. I asked if that was bad and he said 'it's not great', in the voice he uses for the boiler.

So the plan for Saturday is simple. Don't ask if they'll win. Ask who's playing in the middle, because apparently that's where it gets decided, and let him go on about it for as long as he likes. A draw would feel like a win, he says, and I'm choosing to believe him.

Their manager is Daniel Farke. He's German, and he got them promoted. If you need one sentence for the pub, 'Farke's got them looking organised' will do.

That's it, really. Try to look interested when he says Elland Road. It's the ground, and he gets a bit emotional.
