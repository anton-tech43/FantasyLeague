# Argentina — World Cup 2026

Argentina arrive at the 2026 World Cup as the defending champions, managed by Lionel Scaloni (in charge since 2018) and captained by Lionel Messi. The squad retains a large core of the Qatar 2022 winning side and is widely rated among the tournament favourites, ranked third in the world. Recent form has been strong, and they finished top of the South American qualifying group. They are aiming to become the first nation since Brazil in 1962 to retain the trophy. Scaloni has noted that Messi, who turns 39 during the tournament, will not join the squad fully fit after a hamstring issue.

## Key players

**L. Messi** (Attacker, Inter Miami) — Captain leading Argentina into a record-breaking sixth World Cup; will captain the national team for a fourth consecutive tournament.
**Lautaro Martínez** (Attacker, Inter) — The squad's most reliable striker and central to Argentina's attack; was the top scorer at the 2024 Copa América, scoring the winner in the final against Colombia.
**J. Álvarez** (Attacker, Atlético Madrid) — Versatile forward able to play wide, centrally or in a supporting role; netted four goals during the victorious Qatar 2022 campaign.
**E. Fernández** (Midfielder, Chelsea) — Vital midfield presence; won the FIFA Best Young Player Award at the 2022 World Cup.
**C. Romero** (Defender, Tottenham) — Key centre-back from the Qatar 2022 winning side.
**E. Martínez** (Goalkeeper, Aston Villa) — First-choice goalkeeper, known as "Dibu," and a central figure of the world champion side.

**One to watch:** N. Paz (Attacker, Como) — At 21, the brightest of Argentina's new generation; earns his first World Cup call after a breakout Serie A season in which he helped Como reach Champions League qualification, offering creativity behind Messi.
