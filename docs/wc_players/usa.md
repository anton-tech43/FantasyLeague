# USA — World Cup 2026

The USA enter the 2026 World Cup as co-hosts, managed by Mauricio Pochettino, who took charge of the national team in the fall of 2024 after spells at Tottenham, Paris Saint-Germain and Chelsea. Veteran defender Tim Ream, 38 and the squad's oldest player, wears the captain's armband; he led the side in 16 of 23 matches under Pochettino. The 26-man roster blends 13 returnees from the 2022 round-of-16 run with 13 first-time World Cup players, ranking among the youngest squads the USA has sent to a World Cup. Playing on home soil in Group D alongside Paraguay, Australia and Türkiye, the USA are widely viewed as group favorites, with predictive models giving them a strong chance to reach the knockout rounds.

## Key players

**Christian Pulisic** (Attacker, AC Milan) — The team's headline star with 84 caps and 32 goals, though he carried a goal drought into the tournament, having gone goalless for club since December and without a national-team goal since 2024.

**Folarin Balogun** (Attacker, Monaco) — First-choice striker with 8 goals in 25 caps; recorded 19 goals and five assists across 43 games for Monaco in Ligue 1, the Coupe de France and the Champions League this season.

**Weston McKennie** (Midfielder, Juventus) — One of the standout performers of the 2025-26 season, contributing 9 goals and 8 assists across 48 appearances for Juventus while filling multiple roles across the pitch.

**Tyler Adams** (Midfielder, Bournemouth) — Defensive-midfield anchor who made 26 appearances (2 goals, 2 assists) as Bournemouth qualified for the Europa League league phase.

**Antonee Robinson** (Defender, Fulham) — Starting left-back who logged 26 appearances (1 goal, 1 assist) for Fulham in 2025-26.

**Tim Ream** (Defender, Charlotte) — Captain and on-field leader; his calm and positioning steadied a young backline under Pochettino.

## One to watch

**Ricardo Pepi** (Attacker, PSV Eindhoven) — Scored 19 goals in 34 games for PSV despite a mid-season injury that cost him nine matches, helping the club to a third consecutive Eredivisie title and pushing his way firmly into World Cup contention.
