# Morocco — World Cup 2026

Morocco (the Atlas Lions) head to the 2026 World Cup as 2022 semi-finalists — the first African and Arab nation to reach that stage — and as 2025 Africa Cup of Nations finalists, having hosted the tournament. They go into the finals coached by Mohamed Ouahbi, who was appointed in March 2026 after Walid Regragui resigned; Ouahbi previously led Morocco's under-20s to the 2025 U-20 World Cup title and this is his first senior role. The captain is Achraf Hakimi. Morocco are drawn in Group C alongside Brazil, Scotland and Haiti, opening against Brazil on 13 June. Ranked among the world's top sides, they carry genuine ambitions of matching or bettering their 2022 run.

## Key players

**Achraf Hakimi** (defender, Paris Saint Germain) — Captain and Morocco's biggest attacking threat from right-back, he won the 2025 Champions League with PSG and was a leading individual performer at the 2025 AFCON.

**Brahim Díaz** (attacker, Real Madrid) — The creative hub of Morocco's attack, he was top scorer at the 2025 AFCON with five goals in five games and heads to his first World Cup in form.

**Ayoub El Kaabi** (attacker, Olympiakos Piraeus) — A prolific striker known for spectacular overhead kicks, he was among the top scorers at the 2025 AFCON and finished top scorer of the 2024-25 UEFA Europa League.

**Yassine Bounou** (goalkeeper, Al-Hilal Saudi FC) — Known as Bono, Morocco's undisputed first-choice keeper kept three clean sheets in six matches at Qatar 2022 and saved the decisive penalties in the 2025 AFCON semi-final shootout against Nigeria.

**Sofyan Amrabat** (midfielder, Real Betis) — A defensive midfield anchor who provides balance and ball-winning in the centre, a holdover from the 2022 semi-final side.

**Bilal El Khannouss** (midfielder, VfB Stuttgart) — One of Morocco's most exciting young midfielders, he completed a permanent move to Stuttgart and impressed in the Bundesliga; valued for his vision and passing linking midfield to attack.

## One to watch

**Ayyoub Bouaddi** (midfielder, Lille) — At 18 the youngest player in the squad, the Lille midfielder is rated one of the finest young players in France and one of Morocco's most valuable young prospects.
