# South Korea — World Cup 2026

South Korea are managed by Hong Myung-bo, who returned to the national-team role in July 2024 for his second stint after previously leading the side at the 2014 World Cup. Captain Son Heung-min (LAFC) is appearing at his fourth World Cup. South Korea qualified as one of Asia's direct entrants, topping their groups in both the second and third rounds of AFC qualifying and finishing as the confederation's only unbeaten side, reaching their 11th consecutive World Cup. They were drawn into Group A alongside Mexico, South Africa and Czechia, with all three group matches played in Mexico. ESPN's pre-tournament power ranking placed them 30th of the 48 participating nations.

## Key players

**Son Heung-min** (forward, Los Angeles FC) — Captain and South Korea's all-time appearance leader, competing at his fourth World Cup, which he has said could be his last. He was named in FIFA's "World Cup Superstar" selection ahead of the tournament.

**Kim Min-jae** (defender, Bayern München) — First-choice centre-back and a core part of South Korea's defence. He featured in Bayern's 2025-26 Champions League campaign, which ended in a semifinal loss to Lee Kang-in's PSG.

**Lee Kang-in** (midfielder, Paris Saint-Germain) — Creative midfielder who won a second consecutive Champions League title with PSG in 2025-26, joining up with the national team only after the UCL final. He wears jersey number 19 at the World Cup.

**Hwang Hee-chan** (forward, Wolves) — Direct, hard-pressing forward at his third World Cup after 2018 and 2022. He scored a stoppage-time winner against Portugal at Qatar 2022 that sent South Korea into the round of 16.

**Lee Jae-sung** (midfielder, FSV Mainz 05) — Veteran attacking midfielder who made his 100th international appearance in October 2025 and wears the number 10. He recorded seven goals and six assists in 33 Bundesliga appearances in 2024-25.

## One to watch

**Bae Jun-ho** (midfielder, Stoke City) — At 22 he is the only member of South Korea's 2023 U-20 World Cup team to make the senior squad. A two-footed attacking midfielder noted for his passing range and dribbling, he made 45 appearances for Stoke in 2025-26.
