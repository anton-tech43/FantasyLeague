# Iraq — World Cup 2026

Iraq return to the World Cup for the first time since 1986. They qualified via the inter-confederation play-off route: after edging the United Arab Emirates in the AFC fourth round, they beat Bolivia 2-1 in the play-off final in early April 2026. The team is managed by Graham Arnold, who took charge in 2025, and captained by veteran goalkeeper Jalal Hassan. Iraq are drawn in Group I alongside France, Senegal and Norway — widely tagged a "group of death" — and are seen as outsiders to advance, but a side capable of making things awkward for their opponents. Their opening game is against Norway on 16 June.

## Key players

**Aymen Hussein** (Attacker, Al Karma) — Iraq's all-time leading scorer and the focal point of the attack. He scored the decisive goal in the 2-1 play-off win over Bolivia that sealed qualification.

**Zidane Iqbal** (Midfielder, Jong Utrecht) — Manchester-born deep-lying playmaker who came through the Manchester United academy before moving to Utrecht in the Eredivisie. At 22 he sets the team's tempo with his passing.

**Ali Al Hamadi** (Attacker, Luton) — England-based striker and one of the squad's emerging attacking talents, providing a forward option alongside Aymen Hussein.

**Amir Al Ammari** (Midfielder, Cracovia Krakow) — Sweden-raised midfielder playing in Poland who scored a decisive penalty during the qualifying campaign.

**Jalal Hassan Hachim** (Goalkeeper, Al Zawra'a) — The captain and most experienced member of the squad, an Iraq Stars League veteran with around 100 caps.
