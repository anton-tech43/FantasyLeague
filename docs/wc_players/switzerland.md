# Switzerland — World Cup 2026

Switzerland are managed by Murat Yakin and captained by Granit Xhaka. Yakin named a 26-man squad built around an experienced spine, retaining many players from the Qatar 2022 selection. Switzerland are drawn in Group B alongside Bosnia and Herzegovina, Canada, and Qatar, a draw widely viewed as favourable. They are expected to advance from the group, with analysts putting their realistic ceiling at the quarter-finals — the stage they reached at Euro 2024 under Yakin.

## Key players

**Granit Xhaka** (midfielder, Sunderland) — Captain and midfield anchor, set to feature in his fourth consecutive World Cup after Brazil 2014, Russia 2018, and Qatar 2022. At 33 he remains the heartbeat of the Swiss midfield.

**Manuel Akanji** (defender, Inter) — Switzerland's defensive leader, a ball-playing centre-back whose pace lets the side defend high. He joined Inter on loan from Manchester City in September 2025, with reports indicating the move is set to be made permanent.

**Breel Embolo** (forward, Rennes) — Switzerland's main striker and top scorer in qualifying with four goals. He leads the current squad in international goals after overcoming earlier injury problems.

**Dan Ndoye** (forward, Nottingham Forest) — Direct, quick winger who gives Switzerland width and a cutting edge. He moved to Nottingham Forest on a five-year deal in July 2025 and has scored across recent international windows, including in a 4-1 win over Jordan.

**Ardon Jashari** (midfielder, AC Milan) — Swiss midfielder who joined AC Milan from Club Brugge in August 2025 for a reported fee of around €39m, having been named Player of the Year and Young Player of the Year in the Belgian Pro League.

**Ricardo Rodríguez** (defender, Real Betis) — Veteran defender who, alongside Xhaka, Akanji, and Embolo, forms the experienced core of Yakin's squad.

## One to watch

**Dan Ndoye** (forward, Nottingham Forest) — Despite a difficult first season in the Premier League, Ndoye has been in strong form for Switzerland and is tipped by analysts as a potential breakout performer at the tournament.
