# Ivory Coast — World Cup 2026

Ivory Coast (the Elephants) are coached by Emerse Fae, who took charge during the 2023 Africa Cup of Nations on home soil and led the team to the title. They qualified for the 2026 World Cup by topping their CAF qualifying group unbeaten. The squad is captained by midfielder Franck Kessié. At the finals they are drawn in Group E alongside Germany, Ecuador and Curaçao.

## Key players

**Franck Kessié** (midfielder, Al-Ahli Jeddah) — Captain and midfield anchor. He scored the equaliser in the 2023 AFCON final win over Nigeria and reached his 100th international cap in January 2026.

**Amad Diallo** (attacker, Manchester United) — Right-sided forward who brings pace, dribbling and creativity, and is expected to lead the Ivory Coast attack at the finals.

**Wilfried Singo** (defender, Galatasaray) — Pacey, physical right-sided defender who joined Galatasaray from Monaco in August 2025 in a deal worth around €30.8m.

**Simon Adingra** (attacker, Monaco) — Winger who joined Monaco on loan from Sunderland in February 2026, scoring three goals with two assists in his half-season in Ligue 1.

**Seko Fofana** (midfielder, FC Porto) — Experienced central midfielder who, alongside Kessié, provides leadership in the engine room.

**Yan Diomande** (attacker, RB Leipzig) — One to watch. The 19-year-old winger registered 12 goals and 8 assists in his debut Bundesliga season, helping Leipzig qualify for the Champions League, and is among the fastest players in Europe's top leagues.
