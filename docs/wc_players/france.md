# France — World Cup 2026

France enter the 2026 World Cup as one of the favourites, ranked among the world's best squads. Didier Deschamps, in charge for 13 years, confirmed this will be his final tournament as manager — he will not extend his contract beyond the World Cup. He won the 2018 World Cup and reached the 2022 final (lost on penalties to Argentina). Real Madrid forward Kylian Mbappé captains the side at his third World Cup. France are in Group I alongside Senegal, Norway and Iraq. Notably, Eduardo Camavinga was left out of the 26-man squad announced on 14 May 2026.

## Key players

**Kylian Mbappé** (Attacker, Real Madrid) — Captain at his third World Cup; chasing France's all-time scoring record and closing in on the all-time World Cup goals record (reported on 12 World Cup goals).

**Ousmane Dembélé** (Attacker, Paris Saint-Germain) — Reigning 2025 Ballon d'Or winner, named ahead of Lamine Yamal after a 37-goal, 15-assist season in PSG's treble. Expected to be a starter for Les Bleus.

**Mike Maignan** (Goalkeeper, AC Milan) — France's undisputed number one, considered by many the best goalkeeper in the world, expected to start at the tournament.

**William Saliba** (Defender, Arsenal) — A rock at the centre of France's defence and one of the best centre-backs in the Premier League over the last two seasons.

**Aurélien Tchouaméni** (Midfielder, Real Madrid) — Expected to anchor the midfield base for France, likely paired with Warren Zaïre-Emery.

**Michael Olise** (Attacker, Bayern München) — A Ballon d'Or contender competing for a starting attacking berth alongside Dembélé and Mbappé.

## One to watch

**Désiré Doué** (Attacker, Paris Saint-Germain) — A 21-year-old World Cup debutant who won the Champions League Young Player of the Season in PSG's title-winning campaign; brings the freshest legs to the squad. Manchester City's Rayan Cherki (also a debutant) is another emerging option, having recorded 12 assists in his first Premier League season.
