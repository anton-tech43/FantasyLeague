# Jordan — World Cup 2026

Jordan reach the FIFA World Cup for the first time in their history at the 2026 finals. The team, nicknamed "Al-Nashama," is managed by Jamal Sellami, who signed in June 2024 and became the first coach to take Jordan to a World Cup. Defender Ehsan Haddad (Al Hussein) wears the captain's armband, while forward Mousa Tamari of Rennes is the team's standout attacker and the face of this generation. The qualification follows runner-up finishes at the 2023 AFC Asian Cup and the 2025 Arab Cup.

Jordan were drawn into Group J alongside reigning champions Argentina, Algeria and Austria. They open against Austria on 17 June, then face Algeria, before closing the group against Argentina on 27 June.

## Key players

**Mousa Tamari** (forward, Rennes) — The squad's primary attacking threat and only player at a top European club; Jordan's tactical system is built around releasing him into space. He scored seven goals with eleven assists across competitions for Rennes this season and netted seven times in Asian qualifying.

**Ali Olwan** (forward, Al-Sailiya) — One of the squad's most dangerous finishers, he was the third-highest scorer in Asian World Cup qualifying with nine goals, including a hat-trick in a 3-0 away win over Oman.

**Yazan Al Arab** (defender, FC Seoul) — Anchors the backline and is the only K League-based player at the World Cup, providing experience from a competitive Asian top division.

**Ehsan Haddad** (defender, Al Hussein) — The 32-year-old captain and one of the squad's most experienced players, returning after nearly a year out with an Achilles injury to lead the defence.

**Yazid Abu Layla** (goalkeeper, Al Hussein) — The team's established goalkeeper behind a heavily domestic-based, experienced defensive block.

## One to watch

**O. Al Fakhouri** (forward, Pyramids FC) — Odeh Al-Fakhouri, at 19 the youngest forward in the squad, named among Jordan's promising young attackers for their World Cup debut.
