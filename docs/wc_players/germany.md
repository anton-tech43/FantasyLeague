# Germany — World Cup 2026

Germany are managed by Julian Nagelsmann, who at 38 is the youngest manager to lead Germany at a FIFA World Cup. Joshua Kimmich is captain — he plays at right-back under Nagelsmann — with Real Madrid's Antonio Rüdiger as vice-captain. Veteran goalkeeper Manuel Neuer came out of international retirement and is Nagelsmann's first choice for what would be his fifth World Cup. Germany qualified comfortably, winning five of their six group games, and are drawn in Group E: they open on June 14 against Curaçao, face Ivory Coast on June 20, and finish the group against Ecuador on June 25. After group-stage exits in 2018 and 2022, the side blends youth (Jamal Musiala, Florian Wirtz) with experience (Kimmich, Rüdiger, Neuer).

## Key players

**Joshua Kimmich** (Defender / right-back, Bayern München) — Captain of both Bayern and Germany; deployed at right-back under Nagelsmann.

**Antonio Rüdiger** (Defender, Real Madrid) — Germany's defensive anchor and vice-captain, bringing Champions League-winning experience at centre-back.

**Manuel Neuer** (Goalkeeper, Bayern München) — Came out of international retirement and was named first-choice keeper ahead of Oliver Baumann and Alexander Nübel; set for a fifth World Cup.

**Jamal Musiala** (Midfielder, Bayern München) — A central creative force; his interplay with Wirtz drives Germany's fluid attacking movement.

**Florian Wirtz** (Midfielder, Liverpool) — Makes his World Cup debut after a £100m move to Liverpool; missed the 2022 tournament with an ACL injury.

**Kai Havertz** (Attacker, Arsenal) — The Arsenal forward is Nagelsmann's preferred striker option when fit.

## One to watch

**Florian Wirtz** (Midfielder, Liverpool) — Widely flagged as Germany's player to watch ahead of his anticipated World Cup debut. Among emerging names, 17-year-old Bayern midfielder Lennart Karl (#25) is the squad's youngest selection.
