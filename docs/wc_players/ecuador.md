# Ecuador — World Cup 2026

Ecuador head to the 2026 World Cup under Argentine manager Sebastián Beccacece, in charge since August 2024. They qualified by finishing second in CONMEBOL qualifying, behind only defending champions Argentina. Veteran forward Enner Valencia, the country's all-time leading scorer, captains the side in what is expected to be his final World Cup. Ecuador are drawn in Group E alongside Ivory Coast, Curaçao and Germany.

## Key players

**Moisés Caicedo** (midfielder, Chelsea) — Ecuador's standout player and midfield anchor, regarded as one of the leading defensive midfielders in world football after establishing himself at Chelsea.

**Enner Valencia** (forward, Internacional) — Captain and Ecuador's all-time top scorer with 49 goals in 105 caps, including a record six World Cup goals. At 36, this is expected to be his final World Cup.

**Piero Hincapié** (defender, Arsenal) — Centre-back who forms part of Ecuador's defensive core alongside Pacho.

**Willian Pacho** (defender, Paris Saint-Germain) — Centre-back who arrives at the tournament having won a European title with PSG.

**Pervis Estupiñán** (defender, AC Milan) — Left-back who joined AC Milan from Brighton in July 2025 in a deal worth around €17 million, becoming the first Ecuadorian to sign for the club.

**Gonzalo Plata** (forward, Flamengo) — Right winger known for his pace and dribbling, who won the 2025 Copa Libertadores with Flamengo.

## One to watch

**Kendry Páez** (midfielder, Strasbourg) — Teenage attacking midfielder who became the youngest player ever to represent Ecuador and has been widely tipped as one of the tournament's brightest young prospects.
