# South Africa — World Cup 2026

South Africa (Bafana Bafana) return to the World Cup for the first time since 2010, when they appeared as hosts. This is their fourth appearance overall, after 1998, 2002 and 2010; they have never advanced past the group stage. Qualification was sealed by topping their CAF group ahead of Nigeria, their first merit-based qualification since 2002.

The team is coached by Belgian Hugo Broos and captained by goalkeeper Ronwen Williams, the squad's most-capped player. South Africa are in Group A alongside co-hosts Mexico, South Korea and the Czech Republic. They open the tournament against Mexico at the Estadio Azteca in Mexico City on 11 June 2026, the tournament's opening match and a rematch of the 2010 opener. The side relies on organisation, discipline and a young, domestically based core.

## Key players

**Ronwen Williams** (goalkeeper, Mamelodi Sundowns) — Captain since 2021 and the squad's most-capped player. He set an AFCON record by saving four penalties against Cape Verde in the 2023 quarter-final, sending South Africa to the semi-finals.

**Lyle Foster** (forward, Burnley) — The only squad member playing in one of Europe's top-five leagues. A physical striker offering hold-up play and aerial presence, described as South Africa's most important attacking reference point.

**Teboho Mokoena** (midfielder, Mamelodi Sundowns) — The team's midfield anchor, valued for ball-winning, set-piece quality and leadership. He scored in the CAF Champions League final second leg in Rabat.

**Oswin Appollis** (forward, Orlando Pirates) — A left-flank winger whose pace and directness give Bafana a counter-attacking threat. He played all 30 league matches in a title-winning first season at Orlando Pirates after joining from Polokwane City in 2025.

**Relebohile Mofokeng** (forward, Orlando Pirates) — One to watch: at 21, one of African football's most exciting young attackers. Coach Broos handed him the No. 10 shirt, reflecting the squad's shift toward a younger generation.
