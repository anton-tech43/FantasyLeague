# Türkiye — World Cup 2026

Türkiye return to the FIFA World Cup for the first time since 2002, qualifying through the UEFA play-offs. After finishing behind Spain in their qualifying group, they advanced via the play-off route in March 2026 with a 1-0 semi-final win over Romania and a 1-0 final win over Kosovo in Pristina (Kerem Aktürkoğlu scored the decisive goal on 31 March 2026). They are managed by Vincenzo Montella, in charge since September 2023 in his first national-team role, and captained by Inter Milan midfielder Hakan Çalhanoğlu, the squad's most-capped player. Türkiye are drawn in Group D alongside co-hosts the United States, Paraguay and Australia. The side blends an experienced spine with a young attacking core built around Arda Güler and Kenan Yıldız.

## Key players

**Hakan Çalhanoğlu** (midfielder, Inter) — Captain and most-capped player in the squad, a deep-lying playmaker for Inter Milan. He shared the team lead with four assists in World Cup qualifying.

**Arda Güler** (attacker, Real Madrid) — The team's star young talent. In La Liga this season he created the most chances of any Real Madrid player (level with Vinícius Júnior) and led the club in assists.

**Kenan Yıldız** (attacker, Juventus) — Joint-top scorer in qualifying with three goals, and created the most open-play chances for Türkiye (12). Recorded 11 goals and 10 assists for Juventus last season.

**Kerem Aktürkoğlu** (attacker, Fenerbahçe) — Joint-top scorer in qualifying with three goals; scored the 53rd-minute winner against Kosovo that sealed qualification.

**Merih Demiral** (defender, Al-Ahli Jeddah) — Experienced centre-back who anchors the defence. He helped Al-Ahli win the AFC Champions League Elite and is contracted to the Saudi club through 2029.

**Can Uzun** (attacker, Eintracht Frankfurt) — One to watch. The 20-year-old attacking midfielder/forward recorded 10 goals and five assists for Eintracht Frankfurt in 2025/26 and scored in Türkiye's 4-0 pre-tournament friendly win over North Macedonia.
