# Uruguay — World Cup 2026

Uruguay are managed by Marcelo Bielsa, who took charge in 2023 and leads La Celeste into his first World Cup as their coach. His regime is built on relentless pressing, a high defensive line, width and vertical transitions, and he named a squad heavy on midfielders (around 12) with only three recognised forwards. Centre-back José María Giménez (Atlético Madrid) is the captain. The most notable omission was Luis Suárez, who misses a World Cup for the first time since 2010.

Uruguay are drawn in Group H alongside Spain, Saudi Arabia and Cape Verde. They open against Saudi Arabia on 15 June, face Cape Verde on 21 June, and close the group against Spain on 26 June in Guadalajara, a fixture widely seen as decisive. Spain are clear favourites, but Uruguay are well placed to advance from the group's other three teams.

## Key players

**Federico Valverde** (midfielder, Real Madrid) — A two-time Champions League winner and one of the most complete midfielders in the game, valued for his ball-carrying, long-range shooting, work rate and pressing. He is widely regarded as Uruguay's most important outfield player.

**José María Giménez** (defender, Atlético Madrid) — The captain and one of the squad's most experienced players, with around 99 caps. He is expected to anchor the centre of defence and is a key figure in Bielsa's high line.

**Ronald Araújo** (defender, Barcelona) — Captain at club level for Barcelona, a quick, athletic centre-back known for his one-on-one duelling and recovery pace, which lets Uruguay hold a high defensive line. He is expected to partner Giménez at centre-back.

**Rodrigo Bentancur** (midfielder, Tottenham) — A versatile, hard-working central midfielder heading to his third World Cup with 73 caps. He returned in April 2026 after January hamstring surgery, recovering in time for selection.

**Manuel Ugarte** (midfielder, Manchester United) — A combative defensive midfielder who breaks up play with well-timed tackling and positioning, suiting Bielsa's pressing system. One of three Premier League players in the squad.

**Giorgian de Arrascaeta** (midfielder, Flamengo) — One of Uruguay's most creative players, linking midfield and attack. He had clavicle surgery in late April 2026 and later a calf tear, but Bielsa retained him in the squad with a view to him returning later in the tournament.

## One to watch

**Darwin Núñez** (forward, Al-Hilal Saudi FC) — With Suárez omitted and only three forwards named, Núñez is Uruguay's primary striking option. He moved from Liverpool to Al-Hilal in August 2025 in a deal worth up to around €65m, having scored 40 goals in 143 Liverpool appearances.
