# Sweden — World Cup 2026

Sweden reached the 2026 World Cup through the UEFA play-offs under head coach **Graham Potter**, who was appointed in October 2025 on a short-term deal to rescue a faltering campaign. After losing his first match in charge (4–1 to Switzerland), Potter guided the team past Ukraine (3–1) and Poland (3–2) in the March 2026 play-offs to qualify, with Viktor Gyökeres scoring decisive goals in both ties. The squad is captained by veteran defender **Victor Lindelöf**. Sweden were drawn into Group F alongside the Netherlands, Japan and Tunisia.

## Key players

**Victor Lindelöf** (defender, Aston Villa) — Captain and the squad's most-seasoned veteran, he organises the backline. He left Manchester United after eight years and joined Aston Villa on a free transfer in September 2025.

**Alexander Isak** (forward, Liverpool) — Joined Liverpool from Newcastle in September 2025 for a reported British-record fee of around £125m. His season was disrupted by a broken ankle and fibula suffered in December 2025, but he has since returned to score for Sweden.

**Viktor Gyökeres** (forward, Arsenal) — Joined Arsenal from Sporting CP in July 2025 and scored 14 goals in his debut Premier League season as Arsenal won the title. He scored a hat-trick against Ukraine and the 88th-minute winner against Poland to send Sweden to the World Cup.

**Anthony Elanga** (forward, Newcastle) — Signed for Newcastle from Nottingham Forest in July 2025 for a reported £55m, after a Forest season of six goals and 11 assists. He opened the scoring in the play-off final against Poland.

**Lucas Bergvall** (midfielder, Tottenham) — A 19-year-old midfielder who has become a regular for Tottenham since joining in 2024, and was named in Sweden's World Cup squad in May 2026.

**Isak Hien** (defender, Atalanta) — Centre-back for Serie A side Atalanta who partners captain Lindelöf at the heart of Sweden's defence.
