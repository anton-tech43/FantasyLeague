# Czech Republic — World Cup 2026

Czechia return to the World Cup for the first time since 2006, having come through the European playoffs by beating the Republic of Ireland and then Denmark, both on penalties. They are managed by Miroslav Koubek, who took over in December 2025 and at 74 is the oldest head coach at the tournament. Defender Ladislav Krejčí was confirmed as captain in March 2026, taking over from Tomáš Souček. The Czechs are drawn in Group A alongside co-hosts Mexico, South Korea and South Africa.

## Key players

**Patrik Schick** (Attacker, Bayer Leverkusen) — Czechia's standout goal threat with 25 goals in 52 caps. He scored 16 Bundesliga goals in the 2025/26 season and won the Bundesliga title with Leverkusen in 2024.

**Tomáš Souček** (Midfielder, West Ham) — A pivotal presence in central midfield and a long-serving senior figure. He was previously captain before handing the armband to Ladislav Krejčí in March 2026.

**Ladislav Krejčí** (Defender, Wolves) — Confirmed as Czechia's captain in March 2026. The centre-back is on loan in the Premier League with Wolverhampton Wanderers.

**Vladimír Coufal** (Defender, 1899 Hoffenheim) — Experienced right-back with more than 60 caps, a former West Ham player who now plays in the Bundesliga for Hoffenheim.

**Adam Hložek** (Attacker, 1899 Hoffenheim) — A two-footed wide forward who recovered from calf and foot injuries to make the squad. He has five goals in 42 international appearances.

**Pavel Šulc** (Attacker, Lyon) — Versatile forward and attacking midfielder who moved to Lyon and was recalled to the squad during the playoff qualifying stage after recovering from a hamstring injury.

## One to watch

**Hugo Sochůrek** (Midfielder, Sparta Praha) — At 17 he became the youngest player ever to debut for the senior Czech national team, coming off the bench in a 2-1 warm-up win over Kosovo, after which Koubek confirmed his place in the final 26.
