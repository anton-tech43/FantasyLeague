# Bosnia and Herzegovina — World Cup 2026

Bosnia and Herzegovina qualified for the 2026 FIFA World Cup, their second appearance at the finals and first since 2014. They reached the tournament through the UEFA play-offs, beating Wales and then Italy, defeating Italy 4-1 on penalties after a 1-1 draw in Zenica. They are managed by Sergej Barbarez, appointed in April 2024, and captained by record scorer Edin Dzeko. At the finals they are in Group B alongside co-hosts Canada, Switzerland and Qatar; pre-tournament projections rate them behind Switzerland and Canada but ahead of Qatar.

## Key players

**E. Dzeko** (Attacker, Fiorentina) — Captain and Bosnia and Herzegovina's all-time leader in both caps (148) and goals (73) since his debut in 2007. He remains the focal point of the attack and scored six times in World Cup qualifying.

**E. Demirovic** (Attacker, VfB Stuttgart) — Germany-born forward who scored 12 goals with 3 assists in the 2025/26 Bundesliga season. He pledged to buy Stuttgart fans beer and ice cream to celebrate Bosnia's World Cup qualification.

**E. Bajraktarevic** (Attacker, PSV Eindhoven) — Appleton, Wisconsin-born winger who scored the decisive penalty against Italy to send Bosnia to the World Cup. He moved from the New England Revolution academy to PSV in January 2025 and switched his international allegiance from the United States to Bosnia in 2024.

**H. Tabakovic** (Attacker, Borussia Mönchengladbach) — Scored the 79th-minute equalizer against Italy in the play-off final that forced extra time. He netted 13 Bundesliga goals in the season; a late-season ankle injury raised doubts over his availability, though an official diagnosis was reported as pending.

**S. Kolasinac** (Defender, Atalanta) — Experienced defender for the Serie A club. He returned in November 2025 after rupturing an anterior cruciate ligament in April 2025, and reached 100 appearances for Atalanta against Juventus in April 2026.

**A. Dedic** (Defender, Benfica) — Austria-born right-back who joined Benfica from Red Bull Salzburg in July 2025 for around €12 million. He registered 1 goal and 4 assists across Liga Portugal in 2025/26 and can also play on the left.

## One to watch

**E. Bajraktarevic** (Attacker, PSV Eindhoven) — The 20-year-old winger became a headline name after his winning play-off penalty against Italy and represents Bosnia's emerging attacking talent at PSV Eindhoven.
