# Tunisia — World Cup 2026

Tunisia are coached by Sabri Lamouchi, appointed head coach in January 2026 after Sami Trabelsi was dismissed following the AFCON Round of 16 exit. Eintracht Frankfurt midfielder Ellyes Skhiri, the squad's most-capped player, wears the captain's armband. The Eagles of Carthage qualified from CAF Group H without conceding a single goal, and are drawn in Group F alongside the Netherlands, Japan and Sweden. They will be looking to reach the knockout stage for the first time in their history.

## Key players

**Ellyes Skhiri** (midfielder, Eintracht Frankfurt) — Captain and the most-capped member of the squad with 81 caps. A defensive midfielder who controls tempo and is the central figure in Lamouchi's system.

**Hannibal Mejbri** (midfielder, Burnley) — Tunisia's chief creator, typically deployed as a No. 10 for his country. The former Manchester United man brought Premier League experience from a 2025-26 Burnley season in which he recorded a goal and four assists across 25 appearances, and reaches a second consecutive World Cup.

**Montassar Talbi** (defender, Lorient) — Centre-back at Ligue 1 club Lorient and a regular in the Tunisia backline, returning to club duty after featuring at the 2026 Africa Cup of Nations.

**Sebastian Tounekti** (midfielder, Celtic) — Winger who joined Celtic from Hammarby on a five-year deal in September 2025. He has scored two league goals across 22 appearances in his first season in Scotland and netted his first international goal in the build-up to the tournament.

**Firas Chaouat** (forward, Club Africain) — Striker who joined Club Africain in July 2025 and led the Tunisian Ligue 1 scoring charts with 13 goals after 25 rounds in the 2025-26 season.

**One to watch: Khalil Ayari** (midfielder, Paris Saint-Germain) — A young player at Paris Saint-Germain and one of the emerging names in Lamouchi's squad.
