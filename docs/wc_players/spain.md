# Spain — World Cup 2026

Spain arrive at the 2026 World Cup as reigning European champions (EURO 2024) and among the tournament favourites, sitting at or near the top of most outright odds lists alongside France. Luis de la Fuente is the manager and Rodri is the captain, with Unai Simón, Ferran Torres and Mikel Oyarzabal named as additional captains. The side reached the 2025 UEFA Nations League final (losing on penalties to Portugal) and won five of six World Cup qualifiers, dropping points only in a draw with Türkiye. De la Fuente's squad blends youth and experience and, notably, contains no Real Madrid players — a first in Spain's World Cup history. The main concern heading in is fitness: winger Lamine Yamal has been managing a hamstring issue, and Nico Williams' participation has been reported in doubt due to a persistent pelvic injury (pubalgia).

## Key players

**Rodri** (Midfielder, Manchester City) — Captain and 2024 Ballon d'Or winner who anchors the Spanish midfield; he has been working back to full fitness ahead of the tournament.

**Pedri** (Midfielder, Barcelona) — A left-sided creator operating around Rodri in Spain's 4-3-3; the 23-year-old has around 40 caps and scored twice during World Cup qualifying.

**Lamine Yamal** (Attacker, Barcelona) — Spain's breakout star since EURO 2024, since adding two La Liga titles and individual awards; wears the No. 19 shirt and is the player many are most eager to watch, fitness permitting.

**Mikel Oyarzabal** (Attacker, Real Sociedad) — Likely to start as centre-forward; scored the winning goal against England in the EURO 2024 final and is a named additional captain.

**Nico Williams** (Attacker, Athletic Club) — Scored the opener in the EURO 2024 final and was Player of the Match; a key wide attacker whose World Cup availability has been reported in doubt due to a pelvic injury.

**Unai Simón** (Goalkeeper, Athletic Club) — Expected to retain his place as Spain's first-choice goalkeeper and a named additional captain.

## One to watch

**Pau Cubarsí** (Defender, Barcelona) — At 18, a composed, ball-playing centre-back already a regular for Barcelona; reports tip him to partner the experienced Aymeric Laporte and potentially establish himself as one of the game's best young defenders at this World Cup.
