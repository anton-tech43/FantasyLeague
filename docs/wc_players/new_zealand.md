# New Zealand — World Cup 2026

New Zealand (the All Whites) reach the 2026 FIFA World Cup under head coach Darren Bazeley, who has led the side since 2023. They qualified by sweeping the OFC campaign with five wins from five, conceding only one goal. This is their third World Cup appearance (after 1982 and 2010) and their first since 2010; they have never won a finals match. Captain Chris Wood, the nation's all-time leading scorer, leads a squad blending veterans with younger players based in Europe and the A-League. New Zealand are drawn in Group G alongside Belgium, Egypt and Iran, and go in as outsiders as the lowest-ranked side in their region.

## Key players

**Chris Wood** (Attacker, Nottingham Forest) — Captain and New Zealand's all-time top scorer with 45 goals, and the country's most-capped men's player. He scored 20 goals in the Premier League in the 2024/25 season for Nottingham Forest.

**Marko Stamenic** (Midfielder, Swansea) — Wellington-born midfielder who joined Championship club Swansea City in August 2025 on a long-term deal after leaving Nottingham Forest. He scored and assisted in a 2–0 away win at Watford on 31 January 2026.

**Liberato Cacace** (Defender, Wrexham) — Left-back who joined newly-promoted Wrexham from Empoli in July 2025 on a three-year deal. He scored his first Wrexham goal in a 3–3 FA Cup draw against Nottingham Forest in January 2026.

**Tyler Bindon** (Defender, Sheffield Utd) — Young centre-back contracted to Nottingham Forest who spent the 2025–26 season on loan at Championship side Sheffield United, where he made 26 appearances and scored against Birmingham City.

**Alex Paulsen** (Goalkeeper, Lechia Gdansk) — Goalkeeper on the books of Bournemouth, loaned to Polish top-flight club Lechia Gdańsk for 2025–26. He kept 14 clean sheets in 28 league games for Auckland FC the previous season and has represented New Zealand at the 2020 and 2024 Olympics.

**Ben Old** (Midfielder, Saint Etienne) — Winger at Saint-Étienne who has won 20 caps since his 2022 debut. He has featured across wide and wing-back roles and is viewed as an attacking weapon for the All Whites.
