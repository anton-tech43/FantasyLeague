# Belgium — World Cup 2026

Belgium are managed by Rudi Garcia, appointed in January 2025 in his first international management role after club spells at Lille, Roma, Marseille, Lyon, Al-Nassr and Napoli. The squad is captained by Aston Villa midfielder Youri Tielemans. The "Golden Generation" core of Kevin De Bruyne, Romelu Lukaku and Thibaut Courtois reaches a fourth successive World Cup, and the Red Devils enter as clear favourites in Group G alongside Egypt, Iran and New Zealand. Belgium qualified comfortably, finishing top of their UEFA group with 23 points from 10 matches (one loss, two draws), and in the first 2026 international break beat the USMNT 5–2 before drawing 1–1 with Mexico.

## Key players

**Thibaut Courtois** (Goalkeeper, Real Madrid) — One of the world's leading goalkeepers and a two-time Champions League winner with Real Madrid; Belgium's first-choice keeper.

**Kevin De Bruyne** (Midfielder, Napoli) — Belgium's most-capped active player (117 caps at the May 2026 squad announcement), now at Napoli after leaving Manchester City; operates in the number 10 role and dictates Garcia's tempo from central midfield.

**Romelu Lukaku** (Attacker, Napoli) — Belgium's all-time record scorer with 89 international goals, more than 60 clear of any other Belgian, and at a fourth successive World Cup, though he arrived having played limited competitive football this season.

**Youri Tielemans** (Midfielder, Aston Villa) — Captain, organising Belgium's double pivot and balancing aggression with composure in possession.

**Amadou Onana** (Midfielder, Aston Villa) — The midfield enforcer alongside club-mate Tielemans, winning aerial duels and breaking up play as Belgium's defensive anchor.

**Jeremy Doku** (Attacker, Manchester City) — Belgium's most direct wide threat, using pace and dribbling to trouble full-backs; capable of changing matches instantly.

## One to watch

**Matias Fernandez-Pardo** (Attacker, Lille) — A highly rated, uncapped 20-year-old forward providing attacking depth, described in previews as one of the most promising young forwards in the squad.
