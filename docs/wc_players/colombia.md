# Colombia — World Cup 2026

Colombia are managed by Néstor Lorenzo, in charge since June 2022, and captained by James Rodríguez. They qualified by finishing second in CONMEBOL qualifying, conceding just 15 goals across 18 matches, and were runners-up at Copa América 2024. Lorenzo favours a 4-3-3 that shifts to a 4-2-3-1 when defending. They are drawn in Group K alongside Portugal, DR Congo and Uzbekistan, and are widely viewed as a dark horse expected to reach the knockout rounds.

## Key players

**Luis Díaz** (Attacker, Bayern München) — Joined Bayern from Liverpool in July 2025 for a reported £65.5m and scored 15 Bundesliga goals with 13 assists in 2025/26, winning the Bundesliga, DFB-Pokal and German Super Cup. He also scored seven goals in the Champions League campaign.

**James Rodríguez** (Midfielder, León) — Colombia's captain and the Golden Boot winner at the 2014 World Cup, he leads the side from midfield at age 34.

**Daniel Muñoz** (Defender, Crystal Palace) — Crystal Palace's first-choice right-back/wing-back, he won the UEFA Conference League with the club in 2026 and was Colombia's regular starter at right-back through World Cup qualifying. He was a runner-up with Colombia at Copa América 2024.

**Richard Ríos** (Midfielder, Benfica) — A defensive midfielder who joined Benfica from Palmeiras in July 2025 for a club-record €27m, making 23 league appearances in his debut season and winning the Supertaça.

**Jhon Córdoba** (Attacker, FC Krasnodar) — A powerful striker who was the Russian Premier League's top scorer in 2025/26 with 17 goals, helped Krasnodar to their first league title and is the club's all-time leading scorer.

## One to watch

**Luis Suárez** (Attacker, Sporting CP) — Joined Sporting CP in July 2025 and recorded 28 goals in the 2025/26 Liga Portugal season plus five in the Champions League, emerging as a productive option in Colombia's attack.
