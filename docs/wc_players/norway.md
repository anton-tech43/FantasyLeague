# Norway — World Cup 2026

Norway reach the World Cup for the first time since 1998, ending a 28-year absence. The side is managed by Ståle Solbakken, who has been in charge since December 2020 and becomes the first manager to take Norway to a World Cup since Egil Olsen in 1998. The captain is Arsenal midfielder Martin Ødegaard. Norway qualified by winning all eight of their UEFA group matches, and are drawn in Group I alongside France, Senegal and Iraq.

## Key players

**Erling Haaland** (Attacker, Manchester City) — Norway's all-time top scorer, he netted 16 goals in World Cup qualifying to finish as the European Qualifiers' top scorer, scoring in all eight matches. This is his first major international tournament.

**Martin Ødegaard** (Midfielder, Arsenal) — Norway's captain and chief creator, he ended qualifying with the most assists (7) of any player in Europe. He captained Arsenal to the 2025/26 Premier League title.

**Alexander Sørloth** (Attacker, Atletico Madrid) — An experienced centre-forward who scored 13 LaLiga goals in the 2025/26 season, giving Solbakken a second proven striking option alongside Haaland.

**Antonio Nusa** (Attacker, RB Leipzig) — A young left winger who recorded 4 goals and 3 assists in the 2025/26 Bundesliga season, adding pace and dribbling on the flank.

**Sander Berge** (Midfielder, Fulham) — Norway's defensive-midfield anchor, used to shield the back line and break up play in front of the defence.

**Andreas Schjelderup** (Midfielder, Benfica) — A 21-year-old left winger/forward who registered 7 goals and 5 assists in the 2025/26 Liga Portugal season; CIES rated him the second-best under-23 outside Europe's top five leagues.

## One to watch

**Oscar Bobb** (Midfielder, Fulham) — A 22-year-old attacker who joined Fulham in January 2026; his early career was interrupted by injury, but he is rated among Norway's most talented emerging players.
