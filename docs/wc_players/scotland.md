# Scotland — World Cup 2026

Scotland reach the World Cup for the first time since 1998, qualifying after a 4–2 win over Denmark at Hampden in their final qualifier. The squad is managed by Steve Clarke and captained by Liverpool full-back Andy Robertson. Scotland are drawn in Group C alongside Brazil, Morocco and Haiti, opening against Haiti in Boston on 13 June; both Brazil and Morocco were also group opponents during the 1998 campaign in France.

## Key players

**S. McTominay** (Midfielder, Napoli) — Won the Serie A title and was named the league's Most Valuable Player in his debut Napoli season after joining from Manchester United in 2024. Opened the scoring with a bicycle kick in the decisive 4–2 win over Denmark that secured qualification.

**A. Robertson** (Defender, Liverpool) — Captain since September 2018 and Scotland's second-most-capped player ever behind Kenny Dalglish, with over 90 caps. Has led the side at Euro 2020 and Euro 2024.

**J. McGinn** (Midfielder, Aston Villa) — Aston Villa captain and one of Scotland's most experienced players with more than 80 caps and 20 international goals, placing him among the country's top scorers. Captained Villa to the 2026 Europa League final.

**C. Adams** (Attacker, Torino) — Torino forward who featured in all six World Cup qualifiers and scored a hat-trick against Liechtenstein in June 2025. Holds 46 caps and 11 goals for Scotland.

**A. Gunn** (Goalkeeper, Nottingham Forest) — Handed the No 1 jersey ahead of veteran Craig Gordon. Switched his international allegiance to Scotland in 2023 and was part of the Euro 2024 squad. Joined Nottingham Forest in August 2025.

### One to watch

**B. Doak** (Attacker, Bournemouth) — Young right winger who completed a move from Liverpool to Bournemouth in August 2025. Missed Euro 2024 through injury and is part of Clarke's 26-man squad after returning to fitness.
