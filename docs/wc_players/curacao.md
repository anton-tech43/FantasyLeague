# Curaçao — World Cup 2026

Curaçao reach their first-ever World Cup as the smallest nation by population and land area ever to qualify for the tournament (population just over 150,000; around 444 km²). They went unbeaten through CONCACAF qualifying. The team is coached by Dick Advocaat, who guided qualification, stepped down early in 2026 for family reasons, then returned on 12 May after Fred Rutten resigned, and named the 26-man squad on 18 May. At 78, Advocaat is the oldest head coach in World Cup history and the first man to lead three different nations at a World Cup. Midfielder Leandro Bacuna captains the side. Curaçao are the bottom seed in Group E alongside Germany, Ecuador and Côte d'Ivoire, and make their tournament debut against Germany on 14 June in Houston.

## Key players

**Leandro Bacuna** (midfielder, Bandırmaspor) — Captain and midfield anchor. He is Curaçao's second-highest all-time goalscorer and shares the national all-time appearance record (68 caps) with Eloy Room.

**Eloy Room** (goalkeeper, Cercle Brugge) — Veteran goalkeeper and one of Curaçao's most-capped players, sharing the all-time caps record with Bacuna. He kept multiple clean sheets during the World Cup qualifying campaign.

**Tahith Chong** (midfielder, Sheffield Utd) — Born in Willemstad, Curaçao, the winger came through Manchester United's academy before settling at Sheffield United. He switched allegiance from the Netherlands to Curaçao and scored twice in the September 2025 qualifying win over Bermuda.

**Sontje Hansen** (attacker, Middlesbrough) — Direct, pacey wide attacker who plays in the EFL Championship for Middlesbrough. He scored in the 7-0 win over Bermuda in November 2025.

**Jürgen Locadia** (attacker, Miami FC) — Striker who signed for Miami FC of the USL Championship in December 2025. He provided two assists during World Cup qualifying.

**Livano Comenencia** (midfielder, FC Zurich) — One to watch. The 21-year-old (born 3 February 2004) was central to Curaçao's qualifying run. He came through PSV's academy, spent time at Juventus Next Gen, and signed for FC Zürich in August 2025.
