# Paraguay — World Cup 2026

Paraguay return to the FIFA World Cup for the first time since 2010. Argentine manager Gustavo Alfaro, in charge since 2024, guided La Albirroja to sixth place in CONMEBOL qualifying with 28 points (7 wins, 7 draws, 4 losses), recovering from a slow start. The captain is centre-back Gustavo Gómez (Palmeiras), the squad's most-capped player. Alfaro's side is built on a solid defence and quick counter-attacks. Paraguay are drawn in Group D alongside hosts USA, Australia and Turkey, opening their campaign on 12 June in Los Angeles against the USA.

## Key players

**Gustavo Gómez** (Defender, Palmeiras) — Captain and the squad's most experienced player. A commanding centre-back, he anchors Alfaro's defensive setup.

**Omar Alderete** (Defender, Sunderland) — Forms the central-defensive partnership with Gustavo Gómez that is central to Paraguay's organised, counter-attacking approach.

**Miguel Almirón** (Midfielder, Atlanta United FC) — Attacking midfielder who typically operates in a No. 10 role, drifting in from the left; returned to MLS after his spell at Newcastle United.

**Antonio Sanabria** (Attacker, Cremonese) — Central striker who scored four goals in World Cup qualifying despite starting only eight of Paraguay's matches.

**Diego Gómez** (Midfielder, Brighton) — Box-to-box midfielder who drives Paraguay forward as part of their attacking transitions.

**Julio Enciso** (Attacker, Strasbourg) — One to watch. One of Paraguay's most exciting young talents, an unpredictable forward with sharp footwork and a willingness to shoot from distance. He enjoyed a strong 2025/26 in Ligue 1 with Strasbourg and is expected to be Paraguay's main attacking threat.
