# Uzbekistan — World Cup 2026

Uzbekistan reach the FIFA World Cup for the first time in their history, becoming the first Central Asian nation to qualify. The side is managed by Fabio Cannavaro, the former Italy captain who lifted the 2006 World Cup, and is captained by all-time leading scorer Eldor Shomurodov. The team lost only one of its 16 qualifiers and is built on a disciplined, defence-first 3-4-2-1 under Cannavaro. They are drawn in Group K alongside Portugal, Colombia and DR Congo, where they are widely seen as outsiders; the expanded knockout format means a strong third place could still be enough to advance. Uzbekistan open their tournament against Colombia in Mexico City (exact date not fully confirmed across sources), then face Portugal in Houston and DR Congo in Atlanta.

## Key players

**A. Khusanov** (defender, Manchester City) — Centre-back who in January 2025 became the first Uzbek player to sign for a Premier League club, joining Manchester City for a reported initial fee of around €40m. Uzbekistan's highest-profile player and a central figure in their defensive setup.

**E. Shomurodov** (forward, Başakşehir) — Captain and Uzbekistan's all-time top scorer with 44 goals in 90 caps. Led the qualifying campaign with five goals and four assists, and finished his Turkish Süper Lig season as joint top scorer.

**J. Masharipov** (midfielder, Esteghlal FC) — Experienced left winger with 74 caps and 13 goals. A trusted starter under Cannavaro, he provided four assists during qualifying.

**A. Fayzullaev** (midfielder, Başakşehir) — Attacking midfielder/winger who joined İstanbul Başakşehir in July 2025 from CSKA Moscow. Named MVP of the 2023 AFC U-20 Asian Cup, which Uzbekistan won, and scored a brace against Qatar in 2026 World Cup qualifying.

**J. Iskanderov** (midfielder, Neftchi) — Experienced attacking midfielder, aged 32, a regular through the qualifying run.

**I. Sergeev** (forward, Pakhtakor) — Veteran striker with 24 international goals, giving Cannavaro a second focal point alongside Shomurodov.
