# Haiti — World Cup 2026

Haiti (Les Grenadiers) reached the 2026 FIFA World Cup, their second appearance and first since 1974. Their only previous World Cup was in West Germany in 1974, where they lost all three group games. They are coached by French manager Sébastien Migné, who took charge in the summer of 2024, and captained by veteran goalkeeper Johny Placide. The squad is built mainly around diaspora players from clubs in Europe, North America, South America and Asia, with an average age of around 24. Haiti were drawn into Group C alongside Brazil, Morocco and Scotland; their group fixtures are against Scotland (June 13), Brazil (June 19) and Morocco (June 24).

## Key players

**Johny Placide** (Goalkeeper, Bastia) — Captains Haiti at 38, his first World Cup after a long career across France, Belgium and Turkey. He now plays for Bastia in France's lower divisions.

**Jean-Ricner Bellegarde** (Midfielder, Wolves) — Wolverhampton Wanderers midfielder who switched his international allegiance from France to Haiti in August 2025, becoming the first Haiti-represented player to feature in the Premier League. He has become one of Migné's most trusted players in central midfield.

**Wilson Isidor** (Attacker, Sunderland) — Sunderland striker who switched allegiance from France to Haiti after a fan campaign and made his debut in March. A French-born forward of Haitian descent, he is one of Haiti's main attacking threats, known for his pace and physical strength.

**Duckens Nazon** (Attacker, Esteghlal FC) — Haiti's all-time leading goalscorer, instrumental in qualifying with six goals including a hat-trick against Costa Rica.

**Frantzdy Pierrot** (Attacker, AEK Athens FC) — Born in Haiti and raised in Massachusetts, the striker has more than 30 goals for Haiti. He scored two goals in 2026 qualifying, including the only goal in a 1-0 win over Costa Rica, and spent 2025-26 on loan at Çaykur Rizespor.

**Danley Jean-Jacques** (Midfielder, Philadelphia Union) — Defensive midfielder who joined Philadelphia Union from Metz in 2024 and broke out in the Union's 2025 Supporters' Shield-winning season. A regular for Haiti since debuting in 2023, he anchors the midfield.

**One to watch — Wilson Isidor** (Attacker, Sunderland) — Fresh from a Premier League season with Sunderland, the recently converted striker offers pace and power up front and is one of the most promising players of this Haitian generation.
