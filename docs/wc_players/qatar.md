# Qatar — World Cup 2026

Qatar are coached by Spaniard Julen Lopetegui, appointed in May 2025, who led the side to its first World Cup qualification via the AFC fourth round. The team is captained by veteran forward Hassan Al Haydos, Qatar's most-capped player. Drawn in Group B alongside Switzerland, co-hosts Canada, and Bosnia and Herzegovina, the AFC champions are widely rated the group's weakest side and face a difficult task to reach the knockouts.

## Key players

**Akram Afif** (forward, Al Sadd) — Left winger and Qatar's creative heartbeat, named AFC Player of the Year twice. He won the Golden Ball and Golden Boot at the 2023 Asian Cup, scoring eight goals including a hat-trick of penalties in the final.

**Almoez Ali** (forward, Al-Duhail SC) — Qatar's all-time top scorer with 60 international goals and the leading scorer in AFC World Cup qualifying with 12. A sharp penalty-box finisher, he set a single-tournament record with nine goals at the 2019 Asian Cup.

**Hassan Al Haydos** (forward, Al Sadd) — The 35-year-old captain and Qatar's most-capped player, bringing leadership and experience to the squad.

**Boualem Khoukhi** (defender, Al Sadd) — Veteran defender and long-serving member of Qatar's back line, part of the squads that won back-to-back Asian Cups in 2019 and 2023.

**Assim Madibo** (midfielder, Al Wakrah) — Holding midfielder who anchors the centre of the pitch for Qatar.

## One to watch

**Tahsin Jamshid** (forward, Al-Duhail SC) — At 19, one of the youngest players at the tournament and the first footballer of Indian origin selected for a World Cup. Born and raised in Doha to parents from Kerala and a product of the Aspire Academy, the left-sided winger is known for his pace and dribbling.
