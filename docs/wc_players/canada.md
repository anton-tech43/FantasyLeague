# Canada — World Cup 2026

Canada are one of the three co-hosts of the 2026 FIFA World Cup, alongside Mexico and the United States. They are managed by Jesse Marsch, the first American to coach the men's national team, who signed a contract extension through 2030 just before the tournament. Alphonso Davies is captain, with Stephen Eustáquio serving as vice-captain. Canada are in Group B and open the tournament against Bosnia and Herzegovina on June 12 at BMO Field in Toronto, before facing Qatar (June 18, Vancouver) and Switzerland (June 24, Vancouver).

## Key players

**Alphonso Davies** (Defender, Bayern München) — Canada's captain and biggest name, the left-back is regarded as one of the world's best fullbacks and scored Canada's first-ever men's World Cup goal in 2022. He carries a hamstring injury into the tournament that rules him out of the June 12 opener, but Marsch expects him available later in the group stage.

**Jonathan David** (Attacker, Juventus) — Canada's all-time leading scorer with 39 international goals, having become the record-holder in November 2024. He scored eight goals in his debut Serie A season with Juventus after joining from Lille.

**Stephen Eustáquio** (Midfielder, FC Porto) — The vice-captain and midfield anchor, he has been a consistent central presence for Canada since 2019, with 54 caps. His ball-winning and forward passing underpin Canada's pressing game.

**Ismaël Koné** (Midfielder, Sassuolo) — At 23, the midfielder had a breakout 2025-26 season at Sassuolo, scoring six Serie A goals across 37 caps for Canada. His group-stage form is seen as pivotal to Canada's tournament.

**Promise David** (Attacker, Union St. Gilloise) — Canada-born striker who chose Canada over Nigeria and marked his June 2025 debut with a goal against Ukraine. He helped Union Saint-Gilloise win their first league title in 90 years in 2024-25 with 19 goals, and recovered from a February 2026 hip injury in time to make the squad.

## One to watch

**Promise David** (Attacker, Union St. Gilloise) — A late-blooming striker who only debuted for Canada in June 2025, his physical, goal-hungry style at Union Saint-Gilloise could give Marsch an alternative spearhead behind Jonathan David.
