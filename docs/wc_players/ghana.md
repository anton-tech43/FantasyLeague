# Ghana — World Cup 2026

Ghana (the Black Stars) head to the 2026 World Cup under head coach **Carlos Queiroz**, appointed in April 2026 after the dismissal of Otto Addo. Forward **Jordan Ayew** (Leicester City) captains the side and is Ghana's all-time leader in caps. Ghana were drawn in **Group L** alongside England, Croatia and Panama, opening against Panama on 18 June. They arrive as one of the tournament's more unpredictable sides following poor recent results, and will be without Mohammed Kudus, ruled out injured.

## Key players

**Antoine Semenyo** (midfielder/forward, Manchester City) — Ghana's form player and biggest attacking threat. After lighting up the Premier League at Bournemouth he earned a January move to Manchester City, where he scored the winning goal in the FA Cup final against Chelsea in May.

**Thomas Partey** (midfielder, Villarreal) — The experienced engine of Ghana's midfield.

**Iñaki Williams** (forward, Athletic Club) — Brings relentless running and experience up front, having switched his international allegiance from Spain to Ghana in 2022 and become an emotional leader for the team.

**Jordan Ayew** (forward, Leicester) — The 34-year-old captain and Ghana's all-time appearance record holder with 133 caps.

**Kamaldeen Sulemana** (forward, Atalanta) — One to watch. The pacey winger revived his career at Atalanta, scoring his first Ghana goal in five years. With electric pace and dribbling, he could be one of Ghana's most dangerous attacking outlets if his recent form continues.
