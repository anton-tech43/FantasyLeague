# Saudi Arabia — World Cup 2026

Saudi Arabia compete in Group H alongside Spain, Uruguay, and Cape Verde. They are coached by Georgios Donis, the Greek manager appointed on 24 April 2026 after the federation sacked Hervé Renard roughly seven weeks before the tournament; Donis was chosen for his familiarity with the Saudi Pro League, having previously coached Al-Hilal, Al-Wehda, Al-Fateh, and Al-Khaleej. Captain Salem Al-Dawsari, at his third World Cup, is the squad's most experienced player. Projections rate Saudi Arabia behind Spain and Uruguay but ahead of debutants Cape Verde for a knockout place; their best World Cup finish remains the Round of 16 in 1994.

## Key players

**Salem Al Dawsari** (Attacker, Al-Hilal Saudi FC) — Captain and the squad's most-capped player, appearing at his third World Cup with 108 caps and 26 international goals. His goal against Argentina at Qatar 2022 is one of Saudi football's most celebrated moments, and he is the country's joint-highest scorer at World Cups with three goals.

**Saud Abdulhamid** (Defender, Lens) — Right-back and the only squad member based outside the Saudi domestic league, playing in France for RC Lens. He has over 50 international caps at age 26 and is noted for his pace and attacking runs down the flank.

**Feras Al Brikan** (Attacker, Al-Ahli Jeddah) — Al-Ahli striker who was among Saudi Arabia's main goalscorers during World Cup qualifying.

**Musab Al Juwayr** (Midfielder, Al-Qadisiyah FC) — Creative central midfielder born in June 2003 and the youngest player in the squad. He recorded 6 goals and 11 assists in 31 Saudi Pro League appearances in 2025/26, acting as the link between defence and attack.

**Mohammed Al Owais** (Goalkeeper, Al-Ula) — Veteran goalkeeper remembered for his standout display against Argentina at Qatar 2022. Now 34, he spent the 2025/26 season in Saudi Arabia's second division with Al-Ula, recording 11 clean sheets in 35 appearances.

**One to watch: Musab Al Juwayr** — The 22-year-old Al-Qadsiah midfielder is the squad's youngest player and its most productive creator from midfield this season (6 goals, 11 assists), bringing passing range and energy that could shape Saudi Arabia's attacking transitions.
