# Senegal — World Cup 2026

Senegal are coached by Pape Thiaw, who was appointed in December 2024 and led the team through qualification to the 2026 FIFA World Cup. The squad is captained by veteran defender Kalidou Koulibaly. Senegal arrive for their third consecutive World Cup and are widely regarded as one of Africa's strongest sides. They have been drawn in Group I alongside France, Norway and Iraq, a group several previews described as one of the tournament's toughest; predictive models give Senegal a realistic chance of reaching the round of 16 but with little margin for error.

## Key players

**Kalidou Koulibaly** (defender, Al-Hilal Saudi FC) — The captain and one of the most experienced players in the squad, reported to have passed 100 caps for Senegal. He anchors the defence as part of Thiaw's experienced core.

**Sadio Mané** (forward, Al-Nassr) — Senegal's all-time top scorer (reported at 53 goals) and the talisman of the side. At 33 (squad listing), this is expected to be his last World Cup.

**Édouard Mendy** (goalkeeper, Al-Ahli Jeddah) — Senegal's first-choice keeper, who has been a standout in the Saudi Pro League with Al-Ahli, recording a high number of clean sheets in the 2025-26 campaign.

**Nicolas Jackson** (forward, Bayern München) — A fast, physical striker who spent the 2025-26 season on loan at Bayern Munich, where he made 34 appearances and scored 11 goals across all competitions.

**Ismaïla Sarr** (forward, Crystal Palace) — A pacy winger who excelled at Crystal Palace, contributing to FA Cup and Europa League success and impressing in their win over England.

**Idrissa Gana Gueye** (midfielder, Everton) — A vastly experienced defensive midfielder and part of Thiaw's experienced spine, providing balance in the centre of the park.

## One to watch

**Habib Diarra** (midfielder, Sunderland) — An emerging, highly rated young midfielder attracting interest from bigger clubs, viewed as one of Senegal's most exciting prospects for the tournament.
