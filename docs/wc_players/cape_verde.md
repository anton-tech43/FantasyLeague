# Cape Verde — World Cup 2026

Cape Verde reach the FIFA World Cup for the first time in their history, qualifying as one of 10 African representatives. They topped a tough qualifying group ahead of Cameroon and Angola, winning eight of their 10 games. They are managed by **Bubista** (Pedro Leitão Brito), a former Cape Verde captain who has led the side since early 2020, including back-to-back AFCON knockout-stage runs. The team is captained by **Ryan Mendes**, the nation's all-time top scorer and record appearance-maker. Cape Verde have been drawn in **Group H** alongside Spain, Uruguay and Saudi Arabia, where a single point would be a historic achievement; their challenge will be holding a compact defensive shape against superior opposition.

## Key players

**Ryan Mendes** (forward, Iğdır FK) — Captain and the team's all-time leading scorer and most-capped player, leading the line at age 36 some 16 years after his international debut.

**Logan Costa** (defender, Villarreal) — The only squad member playing in one of Europe's top-five leagues and widely regarded as Cape Verde's best player. He ruptured his ACL in pre-season in July 2025 and only returned on 17 May 2026, with Bubista gambling on his fitness for the tournament.

**Jamiro Monteiro** (midfielder, PEC Zwolle) — Experienced central midfielder who scored twice during the qualifying campaign and adds quality in the middle of the park.

**Vózinha** (goalkeeper, Chaves) — Veteran goalkeeper, aged 39, and a holdover from the side that pushed for 2022 World Cup qualification.

**Jovane Cabral** (forward/winger, Estrela) — Attacking option who adds pace and quality in the final third.

**Dailon Livramento** (forward, Casa Pia) — One to watch: at 24 he was Cape Verde's top scorer in qualification with four goals, including the decisive winner against Cameroon and the opener in the qualifying-clinching win over Eswatini.
