# Austria — World Cup 2026

Austria are managed by Ralf Rangnick, who took charge in 2022, and are captained by Real Madrid defender David Alaba. The squad blends experienced internationals with younger talent and is drawn in Group J alongside Argentina, Algeria and Jordan. Rangnick, who guided Austria to the round of 16 at Euro 2024, is widely rated the second-likeliest side to progress from the group behind holders Argentina.

## Key players

**David Alaba** (Defender, Real Madrid) — Austria's captain and biggest name, a four-time Champions League winner (twice with Bayern Munich, twice with Real Madrid). He leads the side despite a limited role at Real Madrid this season.

**Marko Arnautovic** (Attacker, FK Crvena Zvezda / Red Star Belgrade) — Austria's all-time record holder for caps (132) and goals (47). He became the country's all-time top scorer in October 2025 with four goals in a 10-0 win over San Marino.

**Konrad Laimer** (Midfielder, Bayern München) — A versatile Bayern Munich player who can operate at full-back or in midfield, named Austrian Footballer of the Year in 2025. He is expected to be a key man for Rangnick.

**Marcel Sabitzer** (Midfielder, Borussia Dortmund) — A central midfielder for Borussia Dortmund who links defence and attack, adds pressing intensity and contributes goals and assists, bringing big-game experience to the side.

**Kevin Danso** (Defender, Tottenham) — A centre-back who joined Tottenham Hotspur in July 2025 and made 23 Premier League appearances in his first season, also featuring in Tottenham's Europa League final win.

**Nicolas Seiwald** (Midfielder, RB Leipzig) — A defensive midfielder who moved from Red Bull Salzburg to RB Leipzig in 2023, providing balance and control in the centre of the pitch.

## One to watch

**Paul Wanner** (Midfielder, PSV Eindhoven) — A 20-year-old attacking midfielder who came through the Bayern Munich academy and wears the No. 10 playmaker shirt at PSV. After representing Germany at youth level, he switched allegiance to Austria and was fast-tracked into the senior squad, making his debut in a 5-1 win over Ghana. He offers direct, progressive play and genuine creativity.
