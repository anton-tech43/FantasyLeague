# Brazil — World Cup 2026

Brazil head to the 2026 World Cup under Carlo Ancelotti, who was appointed in 2025 and is taking charge of his first World Cup. Marquinhos is captain, with Casemiro a core leader in the dressing room. Qualification was unconvincing — Brazil finished fifth in the 10-team CONMEBOL group, 10 points behind Argentina — but the five-time champions arrive with a deep attacking squad and a favourable group draw (Group C, alongside Morocco, Scotland and Haiti). Bookmakers price them around +750 to win the tournament. Vinícius Júnior is the centrepiece of Ancelotti's project, with Neymar included as a veteran option likely to feature mainly from the bench in what is expected to be his final major tournament.

## Key players

**Vinícius Júnior** (forward, Real Madrid) — 2024 Ballon d'Or runner-up (behind Rodri) and the 2024 The Best FIFA Men's Player; a two-time Champions League winner with Real Madrid, both under Ancelotti, and the focal point of Brazil's attack.

**Raphinha** (forward, Barcelona) — In strong form for Barcelona and one of Brazil's main goal threats from wide positions.

**Marquinhos** (defender, Paris Saint-Germain) — Brazil's captain and a Champions League-winning centre-back with PSG, anchoring the defence.

**Casemiro** (midfielder, Manchester United) — Experienced holding midfielder and a senior leader in the squad, partnering Bruno Guimarães in midfield.

**Bruno Guimarães** (midfielder, Newcastle) — Newcastle's captain who anchors Ancelotti's midfield; this will be his second World Cup after reaching the quarter-finals in 2022.

**Neymar** (forward, Santos) — Veteran included by Ancelotti and expected to contribute primarily as an impact substitute in what is likely his final major international tournament.

## One to watch

**Endrick** (forward, Lyon) — At 19, the Real Madrid-owned striker is making his World Cup debut after a loan spell at Lyon where he recorded eight goals and eight assists. Brazil legend Cafu has tipped him to be the team's breakout star of the tournament.
