# Algeria — World Cup 2026

Algeria return to the World Cup for the first time since 2014, when the Desert Foxes reached the Round of 16. They are coached by Vladimir Petković, the Swiss-Bosnian who previously led Switzerland to the Euro 2020 quarter-finals and took charge of Algeria in 2024. Veteran winger Riyad Mahrez captains the side at what he has described as his last World Cup. Algeria are drawn in Group J alongside Argentina, Austria and Jordan; the stated ambition is to reach the knockout stages, with the squad blending experienced internationals and an emerging younger generation.

## Key players

**Riyad Mahrez** (forward, Al-Ahli Jeddah) — Captain and the team's senior figure, having passed 100 caps for Algeria. The veteran winger is expected to provide creativity and leadership at what he has called his final World Cup.

**Mohamed Amoura** (forward, VfL Wolfsburg) — Wolfsburg's top scorer in the 2025/26 Bundesliga season with 8 goals and 3 assists across 30 league appearances, giving Algeria a quick, direct attacking threat.

**Amine Gouiri** (forward, Marseille) — A forward at Olympique de Marseille who scored during the World Cup qualifying campaign and has expressed confidence in the squad ahead of the tournament.

**Rayan Aït-Nouri** (defender, Manchester City) — Left-back who joined Manchester City from Wolverhampton in June 2025 and won the FA Cup and EFL Cup in his first season, adding attacking width down the left.

**Ramy Bensebaïni** (defender, Borussia Dortmund) — Versatile left-sided defender who can play centre-back or full-back; scored 5 league goals for Dortmund in 2025/26 and is reliable from the penalty spot. He was named in the squad despite a late-season ankle injury.

**Ibrahim Maza** (midfielder, Bayer Leverkusen) — One to watch. Berlin-born midfielder who switched allegiance from Germany to Algeria in late 2024 and joined Bayer Leverkusen from Hertha Berlin for 2025/26, producing 5 goals and 5 assists across 37 matches in his debut season. At 20, he is among the squad's brightest young talents.
