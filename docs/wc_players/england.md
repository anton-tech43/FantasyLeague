# England — World Cup 2026

England head to the 2026 World Cup (United States, Canada and Mexico) managed by **Thomas Tuchel** and captained by **Harry Kane**. England qualified as the first European nation to reach the tournament, winning all six of their qualifying group matches without conceding a goal. The squad blends experience with emerging talent; Tuchel made several notable omissions, leaving out Phil Foden and Cole Palmer. England are rated among the stronger contenders (roughly third-favourites in the betting market behind sides such as France, Spain and Argentina), with the main questions around centre-back balance, knockout-stage pressure against elite opposition, and Tuchel's first major international tournament.

## Key players

**Harry Kane** (Attacker, Bayern München) — England captain and all-time leading scorer with 78 international goals. Appearing at his third World Cup, equalling the record set by Billy Wright; arrives in strong form after a prolific season at Bayern Munich.

**Jude Bellingham** (Midfielder, Real Madrid) — Wears the No. 10 and lines up behind Kane. The 22-year-old had a big impact in England's run to the Euro 2024 final and is expected to be one of the tournament's stars after another strong campaign in Spain.

**Declan Rice** (Midfielder, Arsenal) — Key central midfielder coming off a standout season for an Arsenal side that topped the Premier League and reached the Champions League final. Wears the No. 4.

**Bukayo Saka** (Attacker, Arsenal) — Arsenal winger appearing at his second World Cup, wearing the No. 7. Regarded as one of England's most important attacking players.

**Jordan Pickford** (Goalkeeper, Everton) — England's No. 1, appearing at his third World Cup. A central figure in a qualifying campaign in which England did not concede.

## One to watch

**Nico O'Reilly** (Defender, Manchester City) — One of the breakout players of the Premier League season. The versatile 20-year-old wears No. 3 and looks likely to begin the tournament at left-back. Fellow youngsters Kobbie Mainoo (Manchester United) and Morgan Rogers (Aston Villa) are also tipped as potential breakout names.
