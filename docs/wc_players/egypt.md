# Egypt — World Cup 2026

Egypt return to the World Cup for the first time since 2018, managed by Hossam Hassan, who took charge in February 2024. Captain Mohamed Salah leads the side; Hassan is Egypt's all-time leading scorer (69 goals) and Salah enters the tournament closing in on that record. Egypt were unbeaten in qualifying (W8 D2 L0) and are drawn in Group G with Belgium, Iran and New Zealand. Despite three previous World Cup appearances, Egypt have never won a match at the finals; the goal this time is to end that record and reach the knockout stage for the first time, with most previews tipping them as Belgium's main challengers for second place.

## Key players

**Mohamed Salah** (Attacker, Liverpool) — Captain and Egypt's talisman. He was their top scorer (9) and top assister (3) in World Cup qualifying, directly involved in roughly 60% of their goals, and sits within a few of Hossam Hassan's all-time national scoring record.

**Omar Marmoush** (Attacker, Manchester City) — Forward who joined Manchester City in January 2025 in a reported £59m deal and scored his first career hat-trick against Newcastle. He brings pace and finishing to lead the attack alongside Salah.

**Mohamed El Shenawy** (Goalkeeper, Al Ahly) — Veteran 37-year-old goalkeeper and a dependable presence in goal; he featured at the 2018 World Cup and is set to play again.

**Emam Ashour** (Midfielder, Al Ahly) — Playmaker who provides creativity and energy in midfield.

**Trézéguet** (Attacker, Al Ahly) — Experienced forward expected to support the attack alongside Marmoush.

**Ahmed Zizo** (Attacker, Al Ahly) — Winger who joined Al Ahly from Zamalek on a four-year deal in June 2025; named among Egypt's key attacking options.

## One to watch

**Hamza Abdelkarim** (Attacker, Al Ahly) — Teenage striker (born January 2008) on loan at Barcelona Atlètic from Al Ahly, the first Egyptian to sign for a Barcelona side. A near-uncapped surprise call-up after shining at the Under-17 World Cup, he is the headline newcomer in Hassan's squad.
