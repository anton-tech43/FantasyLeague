# DR Congo — World Cup 2026

DR Congo (the Leopards) qualified for the 2026 FIFA World Cup by winning the inter-confederation play-off final 1-0 against Jamaica after extra time, with Axel Tuanzebe scoring the only goal. It is their first World Cup appearance since 1974, when they competed as Zaire. The team is coached by Sébastien Desabre (in charge since 2022) and captained by centre-back Chancel Mbemba, the side's record appearance maker. At the finals they are drawn in Group K alongside Portugal, Colombia and Uzbekistan, where they are widely seen as outsiders to reach the knockout stage.

## Key players

**Chancel Mbemba** (defender, Lille) — Captain and DR Congo's all-time record appearance maker with 107 caps. A centre-back whose career has taken in Newcastle, Porto and Marseille before Lille.

**Yoane Wissa** (attacker, Newcastle) — A key attacking option who in 2024/25 scored 20 goals for Brentford, becoming the first Congolese player to reach 20 goals in a season in a top-five European league since Shabani Nonda in 2003/04. Has nine international goals from 37 caps.

**Cédric Bakambu** (attacker, Real Betis) — Veteran striker who provides experience up front, with 21 goals for DR Congo, one behind all-time top scorer Dieumerci Mbokani.

**Noah Sadiki** (midfielder, Sunderland) — Box-to-box central midfielder, born in Belgium, who established himself as a regular for Sunderland with over 30 appearances in his debut 2025/26 Premier League season. One to watch.

**Aaron Wan-Bissaka** (defender, West Ham) — Right-back who made his senior DR Congo debut in a 4-1 World Cup qualifying win in South Sudan on 5 September 2025, having previously represented England at youth level.

**Axel Tuanzebe** (defender, Burnley) — Defender who scored the decisive extra-time goal in the 1-0 play-off win over Jamaica that sent DR Congo to the World Cup.
