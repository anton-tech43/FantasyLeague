# Netherlands — World Cup 2026

The Netherlands are managed by Ronald Koeman, in his second spell in charge of the Oranje, and captained by Liverpool centre-back Virgil van Dijk. They have been drawn in Group F alongside Japan, Sweden and Tunisia, and open against Japan on 14 June in Arlington, Texas. The Dutch are widely viewed as a second-tier contender — strong squad and tournament pedigree, but below the favourites Brazil, France, Argentina and England — and are expected to top their group. They carried a long unbeaten run into the tournament but enter on inconsistent recent form. A significant blow was the loss of Xavi Simons, ruled out of the entire tournament with a serious knee injury (Simons is not in this squad).

## Key players

**Virgil van Dijk** (Defender, Liverpool) — Captain and defensive leader; at 34 he remains a commanding, aerially dominant centre-back who organises the back line.

**Memphis Depay** (Attacker, Corinthians) — The Netherlands' all-time leading scorer (reported at 55 international goals), he leads the frontline, though he carried a hamstring injury and had limited recent club minutes ahead of the squad announcement.

**Cody Gakpo** (Attacker, Liverpool) — In strong international form; a versatile forward who can play left wing, central striker or behind the main forward, giving Koeman tactical flexibility.

**Frenkie de Jong** (Midfielder, Barcelona) — Back to form under Hansi Flick at Barcelona; expected to control midfield tempo and progress the ball, likely in a double pivot.

**Ryan Gravenberch** (Midfielder, Liverpool) — Tipped to partner De Jong in a double-pivot role, bringing composure and ball progression to midfield.

**Tijjani Reijnders** (Midfielder, Manchester City) — Joined Manchester City in 2025 after two strong seasons at AC Milan, where he was named Serie A's best midfielder; a key attacking-midfield option.

## One to watch

**Jorrel Hato** (Defender, Chelsea) — A teenage defender already with senior caps, heading to a second consecutive major tournament; widely flagged as a young player to watch.
