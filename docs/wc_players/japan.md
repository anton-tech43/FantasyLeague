# Japan — World Cup 2026

Japan (the Samurai Blue) head to the 2026 World Cup under manager Hajime Moriyasu, who leads the team into his second consecutive World Cup. Liverpool midfielder Wataru Endo captains the side, having become the first international captain to guide his country to qualification when Japan beat Bahrain 2-0. Japan are drawn in Group F with the Netherlands, Sweden and Tunisia, opening against the Netherlands on June 14. The squad is missing several attackers through injury — Kaoru Mitoma (hamstring), Takumi Minamino (ACL) and Hidemasa Morita — but expectations are high for the team to go deep, with a first-ever quarter-final among the stated ambitions.

## Key players

**T. Kubo** (Midfielder, Real Sociedad) — Right winger and Japan's playmaker-in-chief, who scored the qualifying clincher against Bahrain in March 2025 and led the side with 12 goal involvements (four goals, eight assists) in qualifying. With Mitoma and Minamino absent, more attacking responsibility falls on him.

**W. Endo** (Midfielder, Liverpool) — Captain and defensive midfielder with over 70 caps. He had been out of club action since tearing ankle ligaments in mid-February but returned for Japan in a World Cup warm-up friendly against Iceland, playing the first half.

**R. Doan** (Midfielder, Eintracht Frankfurt) — Japan's most dangerous wide attacker, with 64 caps and 11 international goals, including strikes against Germany and Spain at the 2022 World Cup. He recorded 5 goals and 5 assists in the 2025-26 Bundesliga season.

**D. Kamada** (Midfielder, Crystal Palace) — Versatile central/attacking midfielder with 49 caps and 12 international goals. He made 46 appearances for Crystal Palace in 2025-26 and was a joint top scorer (four goals) for Japan in qualifying.

**A. Ueda** (Attacker, Feyenoord) — Clinical striker who scored 26 goals in 40 appearances for Feyenoord in 2025-26 and brings 16 international goals. He leads the line through hold-up play, movement and aerial presence.

**K. Goto** (Attacker, St. Truiden) — One to watch. The 20-year-old forward (on loan at Sint-Truiden from Anderlecht) had a breakout season, sitting among the top of the Belgian Pro League scoring charts. At 6ft 3in he gives Japan a different physical option up front.
