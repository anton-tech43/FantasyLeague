# Iran — World Cup 2026

Iran are coached by Amir Ghalenoei, in his second spell in charge of Team Melli, with winger Alireza Jahanbakhsh wearing the captain's armband. Iran were drawn in Group G alongside Belgium, Egypt and New Zealand. They open against New Zealand on 15 June at SoFi Stadium, then face Belgium (21 June, SoFi Stadium) and Egypt (26 June, Lumen Field, Seattle). Belgium are group favourites; Iran are the second-highest ranked side in the group and will aim to reach the knockout stage for the first time in their history. The squad pairs UAE- and Europe-based attackers with a veteran, mostly domestically-based defence.

## Key players

**M. Taremi** (forward, Olympiakos Piraeus) — Iran's main attacking reference at 33, with over 100 caps and 58 international goals. Reached the 2025 UEFA Champions League final during a spell at Inter Milan before joining Olympiakos; this is his third World Cup.

**A. Jahanbakhsh** (winger, Dender) — National-team captain heading into a fourth senior World Cup after 2014, 2018 and 2022. Former Brighton player and the first Asian footballer to finish top scorer in a major European league (21 goals in the Eredivisie, 2017–18).

**A. Beiranvand** (goalkeeper, Tractor Sazi) — First-choice keeper for a third consecutive World Cup, bringing more than 80 caps. Best known for saving a Cristiano Ronaldo penalty against Portugal at the 2018 tournament.

**S. Ezatolahi** (midfielder, Shabab Al Ahli Dubai) — Defensive midfielder Iran lean on to anchor midfield; reported fit after recovering from a foot injury that kept him out of March's friendlies.

**M. Ghaedi** (forward, Al-Nasr SC) — One to watch: attacking midfielder/forward who joined UAE side Al-Nasr in July 2025 and recorded 4 goals and 4 assists in the 2025–26 Pro League season.
