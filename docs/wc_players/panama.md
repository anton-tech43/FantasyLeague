# Panama — World Cup 2026

Panama qualify for their second FIFA World Cup, eight years after their 2018 debut. The side is coached by Thomas Christiansen and captained by record appearance-maker Aníbal Godoy, with Christiansen keeping much of the experienced core that came through qualifying. Panama are drawn in Group L alongside England, Croatia and Ghana, and open their campaign against Ghana on 17 June.

## Key players

**Aníbal Godoy** (midfielder, San Diego) — Panama's captain and record appearance-maker, with around 157 caps since debuting in 2010, the most in the country's history. A defensive midfielder, he is among the most experienced players at the tournament, having previously featured at the 2022 World Cup and multiple Gold Cups.

**Adalberto Carrasquilla** (midfielder, U.N.A.M. - Pumas) — Widely regarded as one of the best midfielders in CONCACAF and the creative hub of the side, dictating tempo and linking defence to attack. He won the Golden Ball at the 2023 Gold Cup. He picked up a groin issue in the Liga MX final shortly before the squad announcement, though early indications were that it would not rule him out.

**Ismael Díaz** (midfielder, Leon) — An attacking spark in the final third with real acceleration, credited with 17 goals in 53 caps across MLS and Liga MX. He scored a first-half hat-trick against Jamaica at the 2025 Gold Cup.

**José Córdoba** (defender, Norwich) — A 24-year-old centre-back and the only UK-based player in the squad. He joined Norwich in 2024 and made 30 appearances in the 2025-26 season.

**José Fajardo** (forward, Universidad Catolica) — A joint top scorer for Panama during World Cup qualifying, he struck the decisive winner away to El Salvador in October 2025, converting an Amir Murillo cross.

**Amir Murillo** (defender, Marseille) — A versatile, experienced defender now playing in Ligue 1, he provided the assist for Fajardo's decisive qualifying goal against El Salvador.
