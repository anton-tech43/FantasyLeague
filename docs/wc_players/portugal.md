# Portugal — World Cup 2026

Portugal arrive at the 2026 World Cup (hosted across the United States, Canada and Mexico) as reigning UEFA Nations League champions, having beaten Spain on penalties in the June 2025 final. They are managed by Roberto Martínez, the Spanish coach appointed in January 2023, and captained by Cristiano Ronaldo, the national team's all-time leading scorer, who is expected to be playing in his final World Cup. Portugal are drawn in Group K alongside DR Congo, Uzbekistan and Colombia — a group they are widely tipped to navigate comfortably, with reporting framing a deep knockout run (semi-final or beyond) as the realistic expectation for a squad of this depth.

## Key players

**Cristiano Ronaldo** (forward, Al-Nassr) — Captain and Portugal's all-time top scorer; reporting describes 2026 as almost certainly his final World Cup.

**Bruno Fernandes** (midfielder, Manchester United) — The creative hub of Martínez's side in the number-ten role; described as arriving in the form of his life after a season in which he broke the Premier League's all-time single-season assists record (21) and was named Player of the Season.

**Vitinha** (midfielder, Paris Saint-Germain) — The deep-lying playmaker Portugal build around, setting tempo from a double pivot alongside João Neves and freeing Fernandes to play higher up.

**Rúben Dias** (defender, Manchester City) — Portugal's defensive cornerstone; reporting cites his reading of the game and aerial authority as among the best of any centre-back at the tournament.

**Nuno Mendes** (defender, Paris Saint-Germain) — Rated one of the best left-backs in the world; provides width and attacking thrust down the left while defending one-on-one with authority.

**Bernardo Silva** (midfielder, Manchester City) — Listed across previews as one of the squad's core senior playmakers in Martínez's midfield.

## One to watch

**João Neves** (midfielder, Paris Saint-Germain) — At 21, a central-midfield prodigy rated among the best in the world in his position, who played a key role in PSG's back-to-back Champions League success. Entering his first World Cup as one of the most highly rated young players in the game, he is flagged by multiple previews as a potential breakout star of the tournament.
