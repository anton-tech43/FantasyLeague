# Croatia — World Cup 2026

Croatia are managed by Zlatko Dalic, in charge since October 2017, who led the team to the 2018 World Cup final and a third-place finish at Qatar 2022. The captain is 40-year-old AC Milan midfielder Luka Modric, playing in a record-equalling sixth World Cup. Croatia won UEFA qualifying Group L with seven wins and one draw from eight matches (26 goals scored, four conceded), their only dropped points a 0-0 away to Czechia. At the finals they are in Group L with England, Ghana and Panama, opening against England on 17 June in Arlington, Texas. The outlook hinges on the fitness of veteran leaders Modric and Kovacic plus defender Josko Gvardiol, who returned from a broken leg in time for the tournament.

## Key players

**L. Modric** (midfielder, AC Milan) — Captain, record cap holder and primary playmaker; won the Golden Ball as best player at the 2018 World Cup. At 40 he is one of the few men to appear at six World Cups.

**J. Gvardiol** (defender, Manchester City) — Expected starter at the back; fractured his right leg in January 2026 and returned in time for the World Cup after four months out. Plays centre-back or left-back for City.

**M. Kovacic** (midfielder, Manchester City) — Veteran midfielder and one of four squad members with more than 100 caps; his fitness is described as central to Croatia's chances.

**I. Perisic** (forward, PSV Eindhoven) — Croatian legend, 37, who scored in both the 2018 semi-final win over England and the final defeat to France; another of the 100-plus-cap quartet.

**A. Kramaric** (forward, 1899 Hoffenheim) — Ranks third on Croatia's all-time scoring list with 36 international goals and is among the most experienced players in the squad.

**M. Pasalic** (midfielder, Atalanta) — Atalanta regular expected to feature heavily in North America.

## One to watch

**L. Vuskovic** (defender, Hamburger SV) — At 18, the centre-back has been one of the Bundesliga's standout defenders this season on loan at Hamburg from Tottenham, leading his club in aerial duels won, clearances and passes completed. Dalic has singled him out as a player expected to anchor Croatia's defence for the next decade.
