HOW THE STEPS WERE REACHED (simctl cannot tap)
Steps 0-3 come from a truly fresh install (`-gdOnboardingStep N`, no preset).
Steps 4-9 need a club, so they use `-gdPresetTeam <club> -gdOnboardingStep N`. That preset also sets her name to Sophie,
his name to Tom and the tier to 2 ("Came to impress"). So the tier screen shows tier 2 preselected, and every "Tom"
on screen comes from the preset, not from typing.
The season primer is `-gdSeasonPrimer`. A real new user never sees it: completeOnboarding() marks it seen.
After onboarding: `-gdPresetTeam <club>`, then -gdOpenItem / -gdTab N for the first taps. The ATT sheet comes from `-gdForceATT`.
NOT CAPTURED (they need a tap): the three iOS system permission dialogs (notifications, calendar,
Live Activities), and the "One more thing." second-chance sheet that appears if she taps "I'll do this later"
on step 8 (text from NotificationPromptView.swift:73-78, about 40 words, with buttons "Allow notifications" /
"I'll risk missing it"). The ATT dialog IS captured (17-*).

COUNTS BEFORE THE FIRST THING SHE CAN USE
- First piece of content: step 4 "Meet his lot". She gets there after 4 screens, 5 taps and two typed names,
  having read about 66 words (13+8+12+33). Everything before it is a question.
- First usable app screen (the feed): 10 onboarding screens, about 425 words (a) / 404 (b) of app copy, and
  11 taps if she says "later" / "not now" to both asks, or 13 if she says yes to both (each yes adds a system dialog).
  Add two typed names and, if she follows a parent, sibling or friend, one more tap. Then 1 more tap for the ATT sheet,
  which lands on top of the feed before she can touch it.
- Permission prompts before the first usable thing: 2 in onboarding (notifications at step 8, calendar at step 9, both
  only if she taps yes), plus ATT over the first feed screen = 3 system dialogs if she says yes to both. ATT is
  always asked, once, whatever she answered before. The Live Activities ask comes later, at the first live match
  (step 8 pre-warns: "If your iPhone asks about Live Activities, say yes.").
- If she declined notifications, the first feed screen opens with a "Notifications are off / Turn them on / Not now"
  banner. It takes about a quarter of the screen, above the first card (every feed screenshot in this run, because
  the simulator never granted notifications).

WHAT THE FIRST THING IS
The immersive feed, card 1. It shows a lowercase three-beat headline, a one-line dating analogy, "Press for more info and
things to say", and a pink "Your move:" or "Say this:" box with one line to say to him. Card 1 is the newest club news
item. It is not about today's match:
 (a) Newcastle, on match day: "cup game tomorrow / league on monday". It was written yesterday, so on the day of
     the game it still says "tomorrow". Its article (13-*) says "tomorrow night" too. Nothing on the first screen
     says the match is TONIGHT at 19:45. That shows on the club tab's Coming up card, where the time is
     truncated "Thu 29 Oct at 1..." (15-*).
 (b) Liverpool: the League Cup draw (Bournemouth in the quarters), the morning after the shootout win.

WHICH ONBOARDING ANSWERS VISIBLY CHANGE WHAT SHE SEES STRAIGHT AFTER
- Club: everything. Feed, club tab name and crest, Matchday opponent, My Turn "His club, the history" pack.
  The only answer that matters on every screen.
- His name (Tom): visible in onboarding steps 4-6 ("next time Tom starts talking", "The person Tom either loves or
  wants fired", "Tom's team"). After onboarding it shows as "Tom's team" under the crest on the club tab, and in club
  page prose through [his name] placeholders (73 placeholders in team_pages, 6 in content_items over 14 days, e.g. the
  rivalry card "If Tom supports Arsenal..."). It does NOT show on the feed's first screen or on Matchday. The season
  primer says "your boyfriend" even though the name is known. Visible, but only on the club tab.
- Relationship (Partner/Parent/Sibling/Friend): switches he/they pronouns in app copy (AppState.usesHeVoice).
  Not staged here (the preset is Partner).
- Her name (Sophie): CHANGES NOTHING SHE CAN SEE OUTSIDE SETTINGS. Only SettingsView reads herName. There are 0 [her name]
  placeholders in team_pages and 0 in content_items over the last 14 days, and no backend function reads it.
  This is a question we did not need to ask.
- Tier: NOTHING VISIBLE ABOVE THE FOLD ON DAY 1. Pixel diff of Arsenal at tier 1 (screens/L2) against tier 3
  (screens/D1): 18 of the 21 same-name screens are identical. The 3 that differ are two article screens (different
  article ids were used) and the rivalry expand, where tier 3 shows the "Things he doesn't know" insider card below
  the rivalry card. That is a T2+ card, below the fold. The tier also gates sunday_brief items (T2+), the live match
  card (T2+), the Saturday quiz (T3, weekend window only, so not on a Thursday), player dossiers, and which pushes
  she gets. None of that is visible on the first screens on a Thursday.
- Notifications answer: decline -> the "Notifications are off" banner on top of the feed. Accept -> no banner.
- Calendar answer: no visible change in the app (it writes events to her calendar).
