# L2 — 29, primary school teacher, Croydon. Partner is an Arsenal season-ticket holder. Chose "Just enough to get by".

## 1. FIRST LOOK

It's an app that keeps you up to date on your boyfriend's football team in plain English, so you're not the one who missed the big news.

**Maybe, leaning yes.** "Football for partners" and "Football, finally in plain English" are exactly my problem, and I love "No men explaining offside to you". But the store page is out of date, and that put me off. It's late October and the description still goes on about "This summer's tournament", "The big summer of international football kicks off June 11" and "his country". He doesn't have a country, he has Arsenal. I'd wonder whether anyone still looks after it. It also says "No subscription, no ads, no tracking", and then further down there's "Data Used to Track You: Identifiers; Usage Data" and "Third-Party Advertising". Those two contradict each other, and I'd notice. 0 ratings doesn't help. Two things would get me to download it anyway: the screenshot that tells you what to "Drop" in conversation, and the opening screen ("You're here. He has no idea. Let's get you ready."), which made me smile. It's sweet and a bit conspiratorial, and that's the right tone for me.

## 6. THE TEST

At the pub with his mates, the line I'd actually use:

> "Tell him Gyökeres scored but Arsenal still lost on pens to League Two Fleetwood. He'll need a moment."

What I'd actually say out loud: "Losing to Fleetwood on penalties though... you lot alright?" (I'd skip the name I can't pronounce.)

**Smart, mostly.** It's a real, recent, big thing, and I'd be saying it with sympathy rather than pretending to be an expert, so it's safe. I'd feel exposed if someone asked a follow-up ("what did you think of Arteta?"), because the app never told me what he actually did.

**Would I keep the app? Maybe, leaning yes.** I'd keep it for the club tab's "Coming up ... Could go either way", the calendar dots, the live goal pushes ("Ask nothing until the restart") and the occasional "a big thing happened" card. I'd delete it if the lock screen keeps lying (stuck 1-0), if it keeps waking me at 6am about Liverpool, or if I get caught out once by a line like the Saka one. And it needs to actually respect "Just enough to get by": fewer cards, no xG.
