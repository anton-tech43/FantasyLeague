# Panel H: Tom's view. 32, Walthamstow, Arsenal season ticket for 15 years

Read the whole file, plus the one screenshot ("Get to know Liverpool", which is harmless). Shares below are my gut estimates per group: "land" means I'd be impressed or happily drawn in, "wince" means scripted, cliché, patronising, wrong or trying too hard. The rest is neutral, which mostly means I'd nod and move on.

The big thing first: **I'm the one who was at the ground, or watched it.** Any line that starts "Tell him [news about Arsenal]" is her telling me something I knew before she did. Questions that hand me the floor work. News recaps don't.

## 4. Nothing from the app, or these lines?

I'd rather she used it, as long as she picks carefully. About a third of this is genuinely good. The Arsenal history quiz and the in-game "Say This" shouts are what would make me look over and go "where did you get that?". The Lehmann question, "the fourteenth took them a while", the armpit offside line, Kane on 213: I'd love hearing those from her.

But the app also hands her the lines most likely to blow it:
- telling me Arsenal news I knew first
- a question to a season-ticket holder about whether he's been to the Emirates
- asking if an injured Saka is scoring
- calling Fleetwood "a draw"

One of those and I'd look at her phone, and from then on every good line counts against her too. So: use the history, the questions and the in-the-moment shouts. Skip the "Tell him [news]" lines and anything from "His club, right now" or "His squad".

## 5. Patterns

What lands:
- **A question that hands me the floor on something I've argued about for years**: Lehmann, Wenger, Henry or Bergkamp, who covers for Saka. I get to be the expert, and she's chosen a good fight.
- **Something specific, true and slightly obscure**: Fleetwood's Highbury, the 1931 southern-club first, the war years at White Hart Lane, Kane on 213. Obscure beats obvious.
- **A dig at Spurs or a bit of dry humour**: the armpit, the half-and-half scarf, Gunnersaurus.
- **Short shouts in the moment that match what's happening**: the stutter run-up, "not clear and obvious".

What fails:
- **"Tell him [news]".** I'm the source, not the audience.
- **Questions that ignore who I am**: the Emirates, season tickets, "one of the Gooners", "top four is the aim".
- **Templates showing**: the same line for Saka and Ødegaard, "Is he the one to worry about?" three times, "Got it."
- **The wrong mood for the moment**: "flying lately" after Fleetwood, moaning about nil-nil at Anfield, "backs to the wall" at half-time when we're champions.
- **Stage directions about my mood.** Once I've seen them, the whole thing feels like handling.
- **One wrong fact** (Saturday, Chelsea, injured Saka, Iraola if wrong) cancels three good lines.
