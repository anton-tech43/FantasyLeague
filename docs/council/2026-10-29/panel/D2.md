# D2: 28, sales lead, Fulham. Boyfriend (Tom) is Chelsea. Mode: "The one he brags about"

I'm here to beat him: the score before he looks up from the barbecue, and a line that ends the argument. I don't need football explained to me and I don't need to be told how he feels.

---

## 1. FIRST LOOK

**What is it, in one sentence:** an app that turns his club's news into plain-English updates and "things to say", for girlfriends who don't follow football.

**Would I download it: maybe, leaning no.**
- The banner "Keep up. Or wind him up." is the only line on the page written for me. "Wind him up" is my whole relationship.
- Everything else is pitched at a woman who knows nothing: "No men explaining offside to you", "without taking a course". I'm not that woman.
- The description is out of date and it shows. "This summer's tournament is about to make a month of this", "The big summer of international football kicks off June 11", "all 48 nations at this summer's World Championship", plus an England v Croatia example. It's the end of October. If the store page hasn't been touched since June, why would I trust the app to be up to date?
- It says "No subscription, no ads, no tracking", and then the privacy label says "Data Used to Track You: Identifiers; Usage Data". Pick one.
- 0 ratings.
- The welcome screen ("You're here. He has no idea. Let's get you ready.") is fine. It's cheeky, but "get you ready" makes it sound like homework.

## 6. THE TEST

Saturday, pub, him and his Chelsea mates. The one thing I'd say, from the club page Oddity card:

> "…refused to move into Stamford Bridge."

As in: "Fulham were offered Stamford Bridge first and said no. Your club only exists because we turned the ground down."

**Keep the app? Maybe.**

- If it misses one more big result, it's gone.
