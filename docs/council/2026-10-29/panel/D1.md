# D1: solicitor, 33, Islington, lifelong Gooner, mode "The one he brags about"

---

## 1. FIRST LOOK

It's an app that turns your boyfriend's club into short, jokey cards with a line you can say back to him.

**Download? Maybe, leaning no.** "Football for partners" and "No men explaining offside to you" are aimed at me, and I like the tone. Then I read the description. It's late October and it's still selling "this summer's tournament", "all 48 nations at this summer's World Championship", "kicks off June 11" and "a third person in it called England". Nobody has touched the page since June. It also says "No subscription, no ads, no tracking", and two scrolls later the privacy label says "Data Used to Track You: Identifiers; Usage Data". I read contracts for a living, and I would stop at that contradiction. On top of that it has zero ratings. If a friend sent it to me I'd try it. From the store page alone, I wouldn't.

**Keep the app? No.** It doesn't save me time, because I have to verify it. At my tier it mostly tells me what I already know and explains what a red card is. The few sharp things (the drone, the Fleetwood Highbury anecdote, "playing in front of them, not through them", the shootout Live Activity) aren't enough to outweigh being told the Invincibles happened.

## 8. FACTS I THINK ARE WRONG OR STALE

### Definitely wrong (from my own knowledge)
1. **"Ezri Konsa 7'"** as Arsenal's scorer v Everton. Lock screen, Sat 24 Oct 15:08. Konsa is an Aston Villa centre-back.
2. **"Number 41 · 26 years old"** (Declan Rice). Player sheet, TEAM-06. Rice was born January 1999, so he's 27.
3. **"Number 7 · 24 years old"** (Saka). "Who he is" sheet, MY-05. Saka was born September 2001, so he's 25.
4. **"Arsenal and Spurs are neighbours and have been winding each other up since 1887."** The Rivalry card, club page. They first played in 1887, but Arsenal were in Woolwich, south of the river. We became neighbours with the move to Highbury in 1913, and the real bile dates from that and from 1919.
5. **"Champions League – ... Top four PL teams qualify."** Glossary sheet (FEED-07, TEAM-05). This is out of date. England has had a fifth place through the European performance spot.
6. **Offside: "you can't already be nearer the goal than the last defender"**, and the Lingo answer "He was beyond the final defender for the pass". Lingo (MY-07/08/09). It's the second-last opponent (the keeper is usually the last), level is onside, and you can't be offside in your own half.
7. **"Arsenal went on to win five championships in seven seasons during the 1930s."** History fact card, FEED-02. The titles were 1931, 1933, 1934, 1935 and 1938, which is five in eight seasons.
8. **Liverpool on "Saturday"**: lock screen Sun 25 Oct ("Liverpool away next Saturday"), feed card 6 ("anfield saturday", "kept Saturday evening clear"), the Sunday Brief ("Whoever wins on Saturday pulls clear") and the article ("Arsenal have Liverpool away on Saturday"). The club page, calendar and Matchday all say **Sun 1 Nov 16:30**.
9. **"Whoever wins on Saturday pulls clear."** Sunday Brief, FEED-06. We're level with City, not Liverpool. Beating Liverpool doesn't make anyone "pull clear" of the other.
10. **"Drop this: Arteta has been at Arsenal since 2019"**. Club page, The Manager. He was at Arsenal as a player and captain from 2011 to 2016. He's been *manager* since December 2019.
11. **"leaving his dugout zone"**. Feed card 5. The term is the technical area.
