## The first session

In the match week, the worth-it moment came after **≈620 words, 13–15 taps, 2 typed names and up to 3 system dialogs**. At the first feed screen **6 of 6 said "not yet worth it"**. The first screen that felt useful was "Meet the boss" for 4 of 6, and the club tab was where the app delivered for 4 of 6.

**What staging measured:**
- Her name changes nothing she sees outside Settings.
- The tier changes 0 of 21 screens above the fold.
- The tracking prompt lands 1.5 s after she reaches the app, on top of her first card, after a store page that says "no tracking".

**Proposed** (FIRST editor; details in `2026-10-29/editors/FIRST.md`):
- Two screens: Welcome, then his club.
- Land on the club tab, with the full kickoff time, one line to say, the manager and the basics.
- Permissions asked when they pay off:
  - notifications from 48 h before his next match, with the real fixture;
  - the calendar on the Calendar tab;
  - tracking in her second session.
- The result: **≈110 words and 3 taps**. In the worst week (the international break) the landing says so: "No Arsenal for two weeks: it's the international break".

**Ordering caveat** (dependency check): 2.4 is the first build with tracking. Cutting onboarding in the same build leaves no baseline for step-by-step drop-off. This is Anton's call.

**What lands:**
- The goal and full-time pushes, worth the buzz for 6 of 6. L1: "the whole app doing its job in one line".
- The shootout kick by kick on the lock screen.
- The club history quiz: H says about 70% of it would land.
- Say This with its safe and bold lines: all 6 were positive, and M1 and M2 said it is the only part that gives her an opinion of her own.
- The Lingo search.
- "Could go either way".

**What fails:**
- **Wrong facts.** One wrong fact said out loud cancels three good lines (H). These were confirmed by the data and stale seats and by my own checks:
  - penalty exits stored as 1-1 draws, so the calendar shows "D" and the quiz asks "Was the draw with Fleetwood fair?";
  - injured Saka and João Pedro in "ones to know";
  - Howe called Newcastle's manager;
  - "Saturday" for a Sunday match;
  - Saka winning the Ballon d'Or (archived today).
- **Expired time words.** "Tomorrow" and "tonight" stay on cards past their moment, and 5 of 6 were sent in a day late.
- **Lines that manage his mood.** "He'll need a moment" and "give him space" fail. H: once he has seen those stage directions, every question she asks feels like a move.
- **Notification noise.** 5 of 6 objected to competition news pushed to every device at about 06:00; L1 got 10 pushes this week against the 3–5 that Light promises.
- **A store page selling the World Cup** with "no tracking". 6 of 6 caught both.
- **Too much for Light.** 8 quiz packs and 158 Lingo words are not "just enough to get by".
- **Patronising for Deep.** "How many points for a win?" and an underlined "Premier League" come first.

## Confirmed findings, verified by me

**HIGH:**
- **The store description is the World Cup pitch.** It says "no tracking", while the label declares tracking.
- **The privacy URL is broken.** `getgoaldigger.com/privacy` fails the HTTPS handshake, and plain HTTP returns 409. The app's privacy row opens the carrd home page, not a policy.
- **Penalty exits are stored as draws** in `recent_results`, so they read as draws in the calendar and the quiz.
- **Wrong facts in news items**, from fact guards that are missing:
  - the Howe item;
  - "Saturday" for a Sunday match;
  - the wrong table position;
  - two Ballon d'Or winners.

**MEDIUM:**
- **League Cup news pushed to 78–83 devices** whatever club they follow. This path is not in PUSH_RULES.
- **Three finished matches from the outage are stored as `ABD`.**
- **The analytics rate limit counts by the phone's clock.**
- **Delete My Data can leave analytics behind** if she is offline.
- **TestFlight builds log as production.**
- **Ten country followers cannot unfollow**, because `CountryFollowing.isEnabled = false` hides the row.
- **Matchday does not refresh when the app comes back to the front.** Confirmed in code only, not reproduced live.
- **The live box mixes home/away and "his club first".** In an away game, the opponent's scorer sits under his club's name.

**Fixed during the run:**
- The Saka Ballon d'Or item is archived.
- Head-to-head no longer counts friendlies (15101e9). The Newcastle and Everton pages were rebuilt.
- Held League Cup ties show "Penalties" on the app's own live surfaces, and no clock runs during a shootout (a1b30fb, match-watcher v106).
- **Still open:** the app's foreground refresh wipes the shootout note (`LiveMatchSnapshot.swift:59`). That needs an app build.

## Decisions for Anton

The full list, with context, is on the board. In short:
1. Onboarding: cut it in 2.4 or one build later, and should his name and relationship leave the first session?
2. Tracking prompt: move it to the second session? This costs ad-attribution match rate.
3. Notifications: ask at the match? Quiet provisional notifications at the end of setup?
4. Tiers: give them a real effect and move the choice to Settings? Light would get big news only, which needs an item weight.
5. Mood lines: delete everywhere, or keep them for Light? L1 and L2 liked them.
6. Pushes:
   - competition news feed-only?
   - quiet hours until 07:00 London?

7. Sharing paths: delete or fix?
8. Is the Saturday Quiz retired or broken?
9. "Get to know" for Light and Deep?
